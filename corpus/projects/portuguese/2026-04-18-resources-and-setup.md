Learning setup, saturday morning thoughts.

- **Inês** weekly (Thursday), main driver
- **Anki**: deck from the lessons, sentences not single words
- **Podcast** for beginners in European Portuguese (the Brazilian ones are easier to find but I want the Lisbon accent)
- **Duolingo**: keep the streak but it is not the real thing
- **A grammar book** with exercises, only for contractions and verbs
- Subtitles: Portuguese on Portuguese shows, even if I understand 20%

Rule: 20 minutes every day beats two hours on Sunday. Put it after the morning coffee, before Harbor.

Also: the forum had a thread on common mistakes by Russian speakers learning Portuguese. Pronunciation of "lh" and "nh" is not that bad for us, the reduced vowels are easier than for English speakers. Small silver lining.
