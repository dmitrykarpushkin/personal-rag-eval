# Lesson 1 with Inês

Real lesson after the trial call. Zoom, shared doc. Notes:

## Covered
- Greetings: Olá, bom dia (until lunch), boa tarde, boa noite
- "Chamo-me Mara." / "Sou de Tallinn, na Estónia."
- "Prazer em conhecê-la" (formal) vs "Prazer" (casual)
- "Falo um pouco de português." / "Não percebo." / "Pode repetir, por favor?"

## Pronunciation
- Unstressed e almost disappears. "Obrigada" sounds like "brigada" if you rush.
- Final -s is "sh". Hard! I keep saying a Spanish "s".
- Nasal vowels: não, mãe, bom. Tongue stays low.

## Homework
Write five sentences about myself. Ten Anki cards.

Feeling: nervous at the start, much better by minute 30. Inês is patient. She laughed kindly when I tried to roll my r.
