# Ordering at a café

Vocabulary and role play.

| What I want | How to say it |
|---|---|
| espresso | uma bica (Lisbon) |
| coffee with milk | um galão / uma meia de leite |
| water | uma água, com gás / sem gás |
| something sweet | um pastel de nata |
| the bill | a conta, se faz favor |

Phrases
- "Queria um café, por favor."
- "Para levar" (to take away) vs "para comer aqui".
- "Tem wi-fi?" (they all have)

Inês played the waiter, I messed up the order twice, but she said my "bom dia" was already natural. Small victories.

Homework: write a dialogue at a café, 8 lines, include a mistake-free "obrigada". Practise "a conta" aloud.
