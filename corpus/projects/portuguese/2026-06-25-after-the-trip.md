# First lesson after Lisbon

Back on Zoom. We spent half the lesson debriefing the trip.

What I understood: shopkeepers, waiters (slow-ish), Rui when he talked slowly. What I did not: anyone talking to a friend at normal speed, the metro announcements, the older man at the café who spoke in a mumble.

Inês's feedback
- My pronunciation of final -s still too Spanish.
- Vocabulary is OK for A1/A2 survival, but I freeze in the middle of sentences.
- She suggests shadowing: listen to a short clip and repeat it at the same speed.

New words from the trip: "miradouro" (viewpoint), "calçada" (the stone pavement), "rés-do-chão" (ground floor), "andar" (floor / storey), "saudade" (of course).

Homework: shadowing, 10 minutes a day, plus one short voice message in Portuguese to Marta on Saturday.
