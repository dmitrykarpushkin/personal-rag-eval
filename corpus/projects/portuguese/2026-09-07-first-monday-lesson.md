# First Monday lesson (new slot)

The new Monday time. The day after the marathon, legs destroyed, but the brain was fine.

We started with a body-and-sport vocabulary, because Inês asked how the race went.
- a perna, o joelho, a barriga da perna (calf), o pé
- "Estou com cãibras." (I have cramps.)
- "Doem-me as pernas." (My legs hurt.)
- "Fiz uma maratona." / "Corri a maratona em Tallinn."

Past tense practice using the race story: "Comecei bem, mas depois..." She patiently corrected "comecei" (I always say "começei").

Metro text from Inês: a short passage with names of Lisbon lines, which I could read out loud with only three mistakes.

Homework: write the race report in 10 sentences in Portuguese. Voice recording on Wednesday.

Note: Monday evenings at 21:00 my time are fine, as long as I do not plan a late dinner.
