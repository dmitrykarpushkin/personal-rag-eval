Contractions are my nemesis.

em + o = no, em + a = na, em + os = nos, em + as = nas
de + o = do, de + a = da
a + o = ao, a + a = à
por + o = pelo

The rule is simple, applying it live is not. When I speak I stop to think "is it do or de o" and the sentence collapses. I wrote the table on a sticky note, but a table is not a skill.

Experiments:
1. Read aloud 5 minutes daily from a short news piece, focusing on do/da/no/na.
2. Anki cloze cards: "Moro __ centro" (no), "Venho __ Estónia" (da).
3. Say the sentence first, correct after.

Inês recommended not to translate from English at all, think directly in the target phrase. Hard but I get it.

Also: "à" with the accent for a + a, I keep dropping the accent when typing.
