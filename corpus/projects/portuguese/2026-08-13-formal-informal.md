Formal vs informal.

- **tu**: friends, family, people your age, close colleagues. Verb in second person: "Tu falas."
- **você**: tricky in Portugal, can sound distant or even rude depending on context. People often use the person's name or "o senhor / a senhora" instead.
- Formal verb forms use third person: "A senhora quer um café?"

Real life
- To an official or a shopkeeper older than me: "o senhor / a senhora".
- To Marta: tu.
- To Rui: probably "tu" since he uses it with me, but he is a service provider, so keep polite.

Phrases: "Boa tarde, queria falar com a doutora Silva." / "Faz favor." / "Se faz favor."
