Bank and admin vocabulary, since the account is in progress.

- abrir uma conta (open an account), a conta à ordem (current account)
- o cartão de débito, o levantamento (withdrawal), a transferência (transfer)
- o IBAN, o NIF = número de identificação fiscal, "contribuinte" = taxpayer
- o comprovativo (proof, confirmation), o comprovativo de morada (proof of address)
- a morada (address), o código postal
- assinar, preencher o formulário (fill in)

Mistakes: "levantamento" sounded like "levantar" to me, which it is (from "levantar", to lift/withdraw). Cognates help.
