Object pronouns: the European position.

In Portugal the pronoun usually comes after the verb, joined with a hyphen:
- "Vejo-te amanhã." (I see you tomorrow.)
- "Dá-me o livro." (Give me the book.)
- "Disseste-me que ias ligar." 

But before the verb after negatives and some words (que, já, também):
- "Não me disse nada."
- "Já te enviei o ficheiro."

Honestly this is the hardest thing so far. In Brazilian it's simpler: "me dá". Inês insists that I learn the Portugal pattern.
