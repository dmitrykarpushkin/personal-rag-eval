# Portuguese: living project file

Why: I want to handle the landlord, the bank and the café in Portuguese once I'm in Lisbon, not just survive in English.
European Portuguese, not Brazilian — Inês is strict about this ("não é 'você' para tudo").

## Goal (written in April)
Goal: CIPLE (A2) exam in November 2026. Two lessons a month at first, then weekly once the Harbor workload settles.
Lesson notes live under `projects/portuguese/`.

## How I study
- Lessons with Inês Carvalho on Zoom (see people/ines-carvalho.md).
- 15 minutes of Anki every morning, deck "PT-EU core 2k".
- Podcasts on long runs: slow news in Portuguese, two episodes a week.
- Writing: a short diary paragraph after each lesson, Inês corrects it.

## Weak spots

- Ser vs estar, and the personal infinitive.
- Listening at native speed; Lisbon speakers swallow half the vowels.
