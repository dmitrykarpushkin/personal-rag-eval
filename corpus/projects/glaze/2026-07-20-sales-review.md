# Glaze sales review, April–July

Sat down with the spreadsheet that Stripe orders land in and went through the quarter properly.

## Headline
Best seller this quarter: the celadon 'Harbour mug' line, 23 sold. The low bowl was second, quite a way behind. Everything else was one-offs and a couple of gifts.

## What I notice
- Orders cluster right after I post photos on Sunday evenings
- Celadon photographs well in morning light on the window ledge, much better than at night
- Shipping inside Estonia is trivial; shipping to Finland needs extra padding
- Two customers came back for a second mug, which pleased me more than any single number

## Decisions
- Keep the Harbour mug as the front-page item
- Try a second celadon shade in August tests, slightly greener
- No discounting. If it sells slowly I would rather keep it
- Plan a modest autumn batch, then decide about Christmas

Total effort was about two evenings a week, and the studio cost comes off the top. It is not a business yet, but it is not embarrassing either.
