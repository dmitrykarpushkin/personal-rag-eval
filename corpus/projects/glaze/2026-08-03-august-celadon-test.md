# August celadon test

Fired the new greener celadon variants on tiles and two small cups at Savi.

- Variant A: classic with a pinch more copper, soft green, lovely
- Variant B: stronger copper, turned a little blue-grey, pretty but not the Harbour mug colour
- Variant C: thin coat, nearly transparent, a pity

I like A and will make a half batch of it for autumn. The old celadon stays as the main product.

Anu noticed my testing system and said I should write the recipes on the tile backs with the pencil. Done. Each test tile now has a number, a recipe line and a date.
