# Kiln thoughts

I kept circling back to the idea of a small kiln at home. Priced a few, read forum threads, measured the corner of the spare room. Today I actually asked Rebane about it, and the answer made the decision for me.

Home kiln is out: the flat's wiring can't take a 16A kiln and Rebane won't allow electrical work; keep firing at Savi.

Honestly that is a relief. A kiln at home would mean ventilation, fire safety, insurance and a mountain of tinkering. Savi gives me shelf space, Anu's advice and the company of other potters. The membership is the price of not owning a hazard.

Next time I think about it: wait until Lisbon, where a flat with a balcony or a garage might make a different answer possible.
