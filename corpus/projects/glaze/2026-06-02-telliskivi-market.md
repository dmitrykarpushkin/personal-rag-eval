# Telliskivi weekend market

Booked a small table through Anu for Saturday. Brought nineteen mugs and seven bowls, a roll of kraft paper and a card reader.

What happened:
- Slow start, then a rush at noon when the sun came out
- People like to pick up the mug and weigh it in the hand
- Several asked if I ship abroad; I said Estonia and Finland for now
- A woman bought a mug and returned half an hour later for a second one for her mother

Left with a few pieces and a sunburn on one arm. The card reader worked well, and I enjoyed talking to people more than I expected. Will repeat in late summer.
