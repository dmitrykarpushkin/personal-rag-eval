# Packaging notes

Trying to reduce tissue paper and the amount of tape. The current recipe for a mug:

1. Wrap in a sheet of kraft paper, tuck the handle
2. Place in a small corrugated box with crumpled paper around
3. Handwritten card on top
4. One strip of paper tape, no plastic

Test: I dropped a packed mug from table height onto the floor. It survived. The bowl needs a different box because it is wider.

Finland shipments need a stronger outer carton because they go through two sorting hubs. Supplies come from an online packaging shop; ordered the next batch, which arrives next week.
