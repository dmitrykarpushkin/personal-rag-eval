# First glaze test tiles

Made twelve small test tiles from the leftover stoneware and brushed on three glazes, two coats each, numbered on the back with an iron oxide pencil.

1. Satin white — reliable, a little dull
2. Celadon — pale, glassy, pools nicely in the grooves
3. Iron brown — dark, speckled, good for rims

Anu says to fire the tiles on the next free shelf and not to trust any colour until I have seen it on a vertical surface. Dipped the tiles halfway to see the break.

Plan: if celadon works on a real mug shape, make it the main line. Keep the white for bowls.
