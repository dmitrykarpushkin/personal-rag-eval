# Celadon variations

Anu suggested testing how the celadon changes with thickness. Three dips of different duration on ten tiles:

- 3 seconds: almost transparent, a hint of green
- 6 seconds: the classic colour
- 10 seconds: deeper, a bit pooled at the foot, some crazing

I will keep six seconds for the Harbour mug. For August I want to test a slightly greener variant with a touch more copper. Also noting that the clay body matters: the paler body gives a cooler green, the darker one a warmer one.

Photographed all tiles on the windowsill at nine in the morning for reference. The pictures live in the glaze folder with the tile numbers.
