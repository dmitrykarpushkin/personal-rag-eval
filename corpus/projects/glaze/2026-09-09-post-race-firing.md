# First firing after the race

Back at Savi for the first time since the marathon. Walking with sore calves was less fun than working at the wheel, but throwing was good therapy. Loaded two shelves for bisque.

Anu asked how the race went. I said 4:07, and she said "that is longer than my pottery classes" and then apologised.

- 8 mugs, 4 bowls for bisque
- A test piece of the green celadon variant, hand-built
- The new shelves are in use, and the kiln packs much better

I should plan the autumn batch properly. The Christmas question can wait a week.
