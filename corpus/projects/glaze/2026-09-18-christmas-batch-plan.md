# Thinking about a Christmas batch

Is it worth it? List of arguments.

For:
- People look for gifts in November and the shop gets more visits
- The Harbour mug works as a gift, wrapped and ready
- I have the studio rhythm now, two firings a month are possible

Against:
- Harbor work is heavy until the end of the year
- The Lisbon move means a short window after Christmas to ship things
- Not a lot of slack for kiln delays

Compromise: one batch, fired early, with a hard stop for orders on a set date. Decide where to put it in the task list this weekend. Also ask Anu about shelf availability in the weeks before.
