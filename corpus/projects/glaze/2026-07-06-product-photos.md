# Product photo session

Spent a Sunday morning on photos. Setup: kitchen table next to the window, white card as a reflector, a piece of grey linen as backdrop.

What worked:
- Side light, slightly from behind
- Steam in the mug for one shot (a kettle and some patience)
- Hands holding the bowl for scale

What did not:
- Direct sunlight, reflections everywhere
- The telephoto angle, makes the bowl look thick

Edited only brightness and crop, same as always. Uploaded five new images for the Harbour mug page and updated the bowl page. Orders usually come the evening after I post, so we will see.
