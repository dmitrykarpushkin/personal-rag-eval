# Glaze firing, first real batch

Loaded on Friday, fired over the weekend, opened the kiln this morning with Anu standing next to me, which made it feel like Christmas.

- Celadon came out pale and glassy, exactly as on the tiles
- White bowls: lovely, one small pinhole near the rim
- Iron-brown rim: more speckled than expected, I like it
- One mug stuck to the shelf. Wax resist missing on the foot. My fault

Photographed everything while the light was good. Eleven mugs and five bowls look fit to sell.

Anu said to write down every firing. That is what this series of notes is for.
