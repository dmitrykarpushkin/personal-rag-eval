# Shop listing drafts

Writing the first product pages, short and honest.

**Harbour mug, celadon, 300 ml.** Hand-thrown stoneware, glazed inside and out, dishwasher safe but I would not. Each one is slightly different.

**Low bowl, satin white.** Wide and shallow, good for noodles or fruit. Iron-brown rim.

Things I changed after reading it aloud:
- Cut the adjectives in half
- Added exact dimensions
- Put the photo of the mug in morning light first

The name "Harbour mug" came from the colour of the sea on a calm day, and also because my Harbor contract has taken over my brain. Spelling is deliberately different. I do not want people to confuse a mug with a dashboard.
