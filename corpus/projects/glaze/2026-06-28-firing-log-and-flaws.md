# Firing log, late June

Second proper glaze firing at Savi, sharing the load with another member who does tea bowls.

- Result: 9 mugs good, 2 seconds, 5 bowls good, 1 second
- Flaw 1: thin glaze patch on the back of a mug, shows as bare stoneware
- Flaw 2: slight warp in a bowl, it rocks on the table
- Seconds go in the staff box or become gifts

Anu praised the clean glaze lines. She also reminded me to put my initials under the foot so I stop mixing up my work with the neighbour's. Which I do.

New shelves will help the kiln pack better, as she said. It is a small thing but it matters when everyone pays by the shelf-inch.
