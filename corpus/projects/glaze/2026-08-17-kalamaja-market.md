# Kalamaja neighbourhood market

A smaller table than Telliskivi, only a few hours on Saturday, a ten-minute walk from home. Easy logistics: a trolley, a folding stool, a box of newspaper packing.

- Sold mugs and one bowl
- Several neighbours stopped by who had seen the shop link
- A man asked if I could do mugs for his café, I told him to email
- Rain at three, packed up fast

Took photos of the table setup for the shop page. Nice to meet people in person who normally only exist as an order number in the spreadsheet.
