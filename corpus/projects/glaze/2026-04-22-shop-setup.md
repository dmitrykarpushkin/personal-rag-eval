# Shop setup

Finally put the online shop together this week, keeping it as plain as I could.

## Stack
- Simple static site, one page per product
- Checkout through Stripe; shop built on a simple static site, orders land in a Notion-free spreadsheet
- Confirmation email goes out automatically, I send the shipping email by hand
- Photos shot on the windowsill, edited only for brightness

## Why so simple
I spend all day building dashboards for Harbor. The shop should not become another codebase. If it breaks I want to fix it in ten minutes.
