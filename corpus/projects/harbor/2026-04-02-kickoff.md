# Kickoff with Kestrel

First call with Priya and Tomasz this morning (45 min). Jon joined for the last ten minutes.

## What Kestrel wants
A single place where dispatchers see every active shipment: status, ETA, exceptions, who is handling it. Right now they use a shared spreadsheet plus three carrier portals and they lose time every day reconciling them.

## What I heard as priorities
1. Shipment list with filters (customer, carrier, late, exception).
2. Shipment detail with a timeline.
3. Nightly import from their TMS export; live carrier feeds come later.
4. Simple alerts by email when a shipment goes late.

## Agreed
- Weekly sync with Priya, Tuesday mornings.
- Demo at the end of April, a rough but clickable thing.
- Tomasz signs off on anything architectural; mail him a short proposal.
- I start with the data model and the stack choice.

## My worries
- The TMS export is a CSV with unclear column semantics. Need a sample file and someone who knows what "status 7" means.
- Scope will creep. Write down what is out: no customer-facing portal, no billing, no mobile app.

Priya promised the sample export by Friday.
