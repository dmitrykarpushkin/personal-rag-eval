# First demo, feedback

Demo today with Priya and two dispatchers (Lena and a colleague). 30 minutes, 20 of them useful talking.

## What worked
- The list loads fast, and the dispatchers noticed. "Is that live?" (It was the seeded data, but I did not say so loudly.)
- Filtering by carrier. Lena wanted to save a filter. Noted.
- The timeline on the detail page made sense to them immediately.

## What did not
- Too many columns by default. They want ten, I showed sixteen.
- ETA column is ambiguous: is it the carrier's ETA or ours? Needs a label and a tooltip.
- No way to see "what changed since yesterday". They asked for it twice.

## New asks
- Saved filters per user.
- A "changed today" marker.
- Export to CSV (later, not yet).

## Mood
Priya was happy, said the dispatchers have not been this interested in a tool in years. Jon, who watched, will rework the column defaults.

Next: write up the asks as tickets, triage with Priya on Tuesday.
