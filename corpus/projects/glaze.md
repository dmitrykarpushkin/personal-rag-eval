# Glaze — living project file

Glaze is the pottery side business: mugs and bowls, sold online and at the odd market. It is small on purpose. The point is to keep my hands busy away from the screen and to see if it can pay for itself before Lisbon.

## Where I make things
Mara fires her work at the shared ceramics studio Savi run by Anu Põld in Telliskivi; she pays a monthly membership. Savi has two electric kilns and a small shelf area with my name on it. Bisque on one day, glaze firing a week later, usually.

## Costs (spring snapshot)
- Studio: membership €55/month (spring)
- Clay, glazes and packaging bought as needed
- Stripe fees on each order

## Product lines
- Harbour mug — celadon, 300 ml, the one people ask for
- Low bowl — satin white with an iron-brown rim
- Small test pieces — never sold, kept on the windowsill
