# Race day, Tallinn Marathon

Written on the sofa with a bag of frozen peas on my calves.

## Result
Official time 4:07:31. Missed sub-4; walked twice after km 33 (calf cramps).

## How it went
- Weather: cool, dry, a bit of wind along the sea on the way back
- Km 0–21: calm, 5:38–5:42 per km, felt like a jog
- John Kask paced me from km 25 to 35. He stayed beside me on the bike path and did the talking when I could not
- Km 33: right calf locked, walked about a minute. Again around km 36
- Last 5 km: slow but running, finish in the old town with a crowd I did not expect

## Afterwards
Viktor was at the finish and just said "Good. Now rest." Liis will be pleased that the knee held up the whole way. I am not crushed; 4:07 is a real improvement on anything I have run before. But I wanted the 3:59, and I will say so out loud for a day or two.
