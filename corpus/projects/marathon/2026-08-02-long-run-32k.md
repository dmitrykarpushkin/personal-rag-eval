# 32 km, the big one so far

Longest run of the plan. Started 06:30, 32 km along the coast and back, three bottles of water, four gels.

Splits: 6:05 average, last 6 km at 5:50 on purpose. Felt tired but in control. Calves complained on the way home, hips fine, knee fine.

Notes:
- Fuelling worked: no stomach trouble
- Legs a bit stiff in the evening, gone by Monday
- Should practise running in the shoes I will race in; I have only done up to 15 km in them

Viktor was happy with the report and told me to keep the next two weeks boring.
