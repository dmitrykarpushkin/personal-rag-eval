# Two days after

Walking down stairs backwards. Calves are still sore and the quads feel like old wood. No running this week by decree.

What I learned:
- The first half at 5:40 was right, the second half needed more salt
- Calf cramps after km 33 point to electrolytes more than fitness
- John on the bike path from km 25 was the best decision of the day
- 4:07:31 is not the target I set, but it is an honest result

Plan: two weeks of walking and swimming, then a short run with Viktor's group. Think about the next marathon in spring, maybe somewhere warm, maybe in Portugal. Not now.
