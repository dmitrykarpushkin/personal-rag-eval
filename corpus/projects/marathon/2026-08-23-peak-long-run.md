# Peak long run, 34 km

The last big one. 34 km, start 06:00, sunny and cool. Ran Kalamaja, Pirita, Viimsi and back.

Pace 6:03, finishing at 5:48. At km 30 I got a brief thought of quitting, then a gel arrived and it vanished.

Legs: tired, no pain. Knee: perfect. Shoes: the new pair, no blisters.

Viktor calls the next three weeks the "reward". I call it nerves. The plan sheet says taper from next Sunday. I stood on the balcony after the run for half an hour doing nothing, which is rare.
