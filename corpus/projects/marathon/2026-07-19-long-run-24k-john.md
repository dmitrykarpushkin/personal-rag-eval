# 24 km with John

John was in Tallinn for a bike fair and we ran together on Sunday morning. He did 10 km with me, then waited at a café with the paper while I finished the loop.

- Pace: 6:00 to 5:55, negative split
- Knee: nothing at all
- Gels at 8, 15 and 21
- Weather: warm and a bit humid

He told me about a customer who wanted a bike with "no gears but also no pedals". Laughed for 3 km. Good morale boost. I told him I might ask him to run part of the race with me. He said he would think about it, which means yes.
