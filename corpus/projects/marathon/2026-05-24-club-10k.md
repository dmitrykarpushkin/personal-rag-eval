# Club 10k time trial

Viktor's club 10k at Kadriorg, treated as a hard training day as he told us. Ran 49:12, which works out to 4:55 per km. Best 10k I have done.

- First 5 km a bit too hot, second half held on
- Felt strong enough to run another few kilometres, a good sign
- Viktor's comment: "Marathon pace will feel like a walk compared with this. Do not forget it."

Afterwards: coffee with the group, then home and a nap. Put 49:12 in the training sheet. I will not repeat the test; the next real test is the long runs.
