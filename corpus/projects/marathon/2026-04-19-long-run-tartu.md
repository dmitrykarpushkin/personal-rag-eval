# Long run in Tartu, 22 km

Took the early bus to Tartu and ran along the river with John Kask. He cycled the first 8 km on a bike from the shop, then ran the middle section in his old trainers and swore at me affectionately.

- Route: Emajõgi loop out toward the airport road and back
- Pace: 6:10 per km, comfortably slow
- Fuel: one gel at km 12, a banana before the start
- Legs: heavy at km 18, fine after a stop for water

John said I looked like someone who would finish. He says that to everyone. Lunch afterwards was soup and far too much bread. Back in Tallinn by 18:00 and slept like a rock.
