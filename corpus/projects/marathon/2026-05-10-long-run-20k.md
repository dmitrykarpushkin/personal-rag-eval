# 20 km long run and the shoe question

Sunday, sunny, 12 degrees. Ran Kalamaja to Pirita and back, 20 km, average 6:02.

Good: steady breathing, no stitch, first time I felt "this is just what I do on Sundays".
Not good: right foot went numb around km 15. Probably laced too tight.

Shoe decision: I will use the current pair for the long runs and buy the next pair in July, break them in over two weeks, then leave them alone. Rule from Viktor: no new shoes in the last three weeks.

Gels: tried a lemon one, tolerable, a bit sticky. Will compare with another brand next time.
