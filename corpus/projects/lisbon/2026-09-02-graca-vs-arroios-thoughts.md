Can't stop thinking about the two flats.

Graça: heart. The view is the thing I will remember. Tiny kitchen but who cares.
Arroios: head. Bigger room for a studio, near Marta, closer to the metro.

I asked Rui about the stairs in Graça and he said it is "a good workout". Not helpful. I wrote to Marta asking whether she knows the Arroios street, and she said it is fine at night, one bar on the corner but it closes early.

Called Oskar, he said: decide as if you will live there for the first year only, then optimise. Wise, but not decisive.

Test I came up with: imagine carrying a 25 kg bag of clay up to each flat. That settles half of it. Will write the pros and cons properly this weekend and decide by the middle of the month.

Also marathon is on Sunday, so this week is for tapering, not for big decisions. Annoying overlap.
