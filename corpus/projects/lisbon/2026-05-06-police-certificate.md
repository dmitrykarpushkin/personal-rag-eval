# Criminal record certificate

Applied online for the Estonian criminal record extract today. Rui wants it apostilled and probably translated, so I picked the option for use abroad.

Steps as I understand them:
1. Request the extract with my ID card (done).
2. Wait for the PDF / paper copy.
3. Apostille from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs.
4. Certified translation into English or Portuguese. Rui has a translator he trusts.

Timing matters: these documents expire for visa purposes after a few months, so I should not get them too early. Rui said to start about three months before applying. I am a little early but would rather be early than rush.
