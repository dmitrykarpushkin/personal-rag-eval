# Lisbon trip notes (19-24 June)

Writing this on the last full evening. Lisbon trip June 19-24: met Rui (viewed 3 flats), coffee with Marta at the co-working space, first in-person lesson with Inês.

## Day by day
- Fri 19: landed late, walked around Arroios in the dark; felt immediately at home. Slept badly because of the heat.
- Sat 20: flat viewings with Rui. He drives fast and talks faster. Three flats, one in Graça, with a view that stopped me in my tracks.
- Sun 21: Alfama on foot, too many tourists but the tiles are unreal. Fish dinner.
- Mon 22: first in-person lesson with Inês at a café near her place. She is exactly as she sounds on Zoom, only taller than expected. Afterwards coffee with Marta at the co-working space; she introduced me to two freelancers.
- Tue 23: laptop day from a café, Harbor tickets.
- Wed 24: back home.

## Impressions
- Hills are real. My knee complained (IT band, see health).
- Pastel de nata count: lost track.
- The city feels doable for me, even without much Portuguese yet.
