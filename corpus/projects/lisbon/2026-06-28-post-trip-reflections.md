Back for four days now and the trip keeps replaying.

What worked: walking everywhere, the neighbourhood feel in Arroios, how nice Marta was, how patient Inês is in person. What was harder than expected: hearing real speech. People speak fast and swallow half the word. My Zoom Portuguese felt like a lab version.

Flats: Rui's three viewings gave me a feel for the price/quality trade-off. One was lovely but dark, one had the view, one was practical. We wrote down what each was missing. This helps to refine the criteria list (see the flat criteria note when I write it).

What I am afraid of
- The paperwork chain (NIF, bank, lease, visa) and the waiting.
- Losing clients by being in a different time zone. Kestrel is fine with it, I think; Lisbon is only two hours behind Tallinn.
- Finding a studio for pottery.

What I am sure of: I want this. Next steps are NIF with Rui, then the bank.

Also told Oskar, he was more enthusiastic than my mum will be.
