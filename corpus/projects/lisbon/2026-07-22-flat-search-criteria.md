# What I want from the flat

After the June viewings I know my priorities better.

## Must
- Two rooms, so one can be a studio
- Reasonable internet (fibre)
- Quiet bedroom side
- Walking distance to a metro station
- A landlord who will do a normal contract Rui can review

## Nice
- Balcony, even a tiny one
- Washing machine
- Elevator, because of the clay bags and later heavy boxes (still unsure how much that matters)
- Light

## No
- Ground floor on a busy street
- Gas heating problems, I read that some flats have poor insulation. Winter indoors can be cold.

## Neighbourhoods on the list
Arroios, Graça, a maybe for Campo de Ourique. Marta says Arroios is lively, with international people and cheap food.

Rui will send a shortlist when the August listings update.
