# Health insurance for the visa

Compared the options I could find:

- **Travel-style policy**: cheapest, but the consulate may not accept it for a long stay.
- **International health plan**: more expensive, covers private clinics, accepted by most consulates.
- **Local private plan** after arrival: not available before I have residency, apparently.

What the visa file needs: coverage in Portugal for the whole initial period, medical expenses and repatriation, proof as a PDF with my name.

Plan: go with an international plan for the first year, then move to a Portuguese private plan once I have the residence card. SNS (public system) enrolment comes after the residence permit.

I asked Dr. Saar's office if they have an English letter listing my dental history; may help with some plans. Also remember Estonian health insurance fund (Haigekassa) status will change when I leave.
