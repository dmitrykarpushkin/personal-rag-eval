# Flat decision

Decision made tonight, after two weeks of going back and forth. Signed for the Arroios flat (Rua Morais Soares, 2 rooms, €1,350/month) starting 2027-01-15; dropped the Graça one because of the stairs and no lift.

- Second room becomes my studio, which matters as I keep doing Harbor work.
