Utilities, early research. Nothing signed.

- Internet: Rui says fibre providers need a NIF and a contract in my name; installation takes a week or two. Order it a few weeks before arrival so it works the day I land. Compare three providers when I have time.
- Electricity and gas: in Portugal contracts are separate from rent, usually with the tenant. Check whether the flat has gas.
- Water: handled by the municipality, with an account in my name.
- Mobile plan: take a Portuguese prepaid first, and keep the Estonian number alive through roaming for the transition.
- Waste, condominium fee: usually in the rent or paid by the landlord, check the contract.

Order of operations
1. Bank account is finished in October.
2. Contracts after the move date is certain.
3. Switch Estonian services off at the end.
