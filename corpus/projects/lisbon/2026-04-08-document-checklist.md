# D8 document checklist (working draft)

Collected from three sources; to be double-checked with Rui.

- [ ] valid passport (check expiry, remaining validity)
- [ ] two passport photos
- [ ] proof of remote-work income: contracts, invoices, bank statements
- [ ] tax residency and tax status evidence (Kadri may help with the Estonian side)
- [ ] criminal record certificate from Estonia, apostilled
- [ ] health insurance covering Portugal
- [ ] proof of accommodation in Portugal
- [ ] Portuguese NIF
- [ ] Portuguese bank account
- [ ] application form
- [ ] visa fee

Notes
- Everything not in Portuguese or English probably needs a certified translation. Estonian documents: yes, probably.
- The accommodation proof is the circular part: lease needs NIF, NIF needs an address or a fiscal representative, bank account needs NIF.
- Order of attack: NIF first, then bank, then flat, then rest.
