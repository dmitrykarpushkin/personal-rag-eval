# Visa route research

Spent the evening reading about ways to live in Portugal as a non-EU freelancer. I want the paperwork clean and the route clearly documented, whatever the final category turns out to be.

What I compared:
- **D8 digital nomad visa**: designed for remote workers with income from outside Portugal. Needs proof of regular income, a contract or client evidence, accommodation, insurance.
- **D7**: passive income route, not a fit for me.
- **Job-seeker visa**: no.

Verdict: D8 looks right. The official wording talks about "remote work" with an employer or as self-employed with several clients. I have the Kestrel contract plus Glaze sales, so the evidence is there if I keep my paperwork tidy.

Open points
- Does the income threshold apply per month for the last few months? (ask a human)
- Where to apply from
- How long a visa takes to turn into a residence permit

Found a forum thread with a lot of horror stories about waiting times. Not panicking yet, I have time.
