# Marathon — living project file

Goal race: Tallinn Marathon on 2026-09-06, target under 4 hours.

## Why
Sub-4 has been the running goal for two years. A 4:00 marathon is 5:41 per km, which feels possible on a good day and silly on a bad one.

## Structure
- 22 weeks, starting early April
- Club sessions with Viktor Volkov on Tuesdays and Thursdays (Kalamaja Runners)
- Long run on Sundays, sometimes with John Kask when he is in town or I take the bus to Tartu
- Strength and mobility twice a week, loosely
- Peak weeks in July–August, then a three-week taper

## Rules I set myself
1. Easy days actually easy.
2. No new shoes in the last three weeks.
3. Practise fuelling on every long run over 18 km.
4. If something hurts for more than three days, see Liis.

Weekly logs and long-run notes are in `projects/marathon/`.
