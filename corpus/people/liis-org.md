# Liis Org

Physiotherapist, small clinic in Kalamaja, ten minutes from home. Recommended by Viktor's running club, and half of the club already goes to her.

## Style
- Tests first, talks afterward
- Gives short, specific exercise lists and asks for feedback
- Does not like unnecessary tape or gadgets
- Believes strength work beats stretching for runners

## History with me
- First visit in June as prevention before the peak weeks
- Treated the right knee problem in summer
- Checks calves and hips before each race phase
- Recovery visit after the marathon

Keep: book a session when the next training block starts.
