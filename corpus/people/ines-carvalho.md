# Inês Carvalho

Portuguese tutor (online), based in Lisbon. **Inês Carvalho, Portuguese tutor, lessons over Zoom; met through a Lisbon expat forum in April 2026.**

## How we found each other
- Posted in the expat forum thread about learning European Portuguese rather than the Brazilian variety. Inês replied within a day, we did a 20-minute trial call, and I booked her on the spot.
- She teaches in a mix: mostly Portuguese, English when I am really stuck. No Russian, which is fine.
- She is a friend of Marta Sousa (see people/marta-sousa.md), which is how I ended up with a Lisbon contact before ever landing there.

## Format
- Lessons on Thursdays at 18:00 (original schedule). 60 minutes, Zoom, she shares a Google Doc where she types corrections live.
- Homework is usually one short written paragraph plus ~20 Anki cards.
- She insists on European pronunciation: reduced unstressed vowels, the "sh" sound of final -s. I am slowly getting there.

## Things she keeps correcting
- ser vs estar (I overuse estar)
- em + article contractions (no, na, nos, nas)
- "estar a + infinitive" instead of the gerund
