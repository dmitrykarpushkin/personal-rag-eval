# Rui Mendes

Relocation agent in Lisbon, helps with NIF, bank account and flat viewings; fixed fee package. I found him through the same expat forum where I found Inês. English is perfect, replies to WhatsApp and email the same day, sometimes at odd hours.

## What the package covers
- NIF via his office acting as fiscal representative
- support opening a Portuguese bank account
- flat search and accompanied viewings (also by video)
- checking the lease before I sign

It is a fixed fee, so I do not need to track hours or worry about extras creeping in. Payment schedule agreed by email, noted in the money folder.

## Not covered
- The visa application itself: he points me at the right documents but I submit them. He said a lawyer is only needed if something is unusual.
- Moving company.
