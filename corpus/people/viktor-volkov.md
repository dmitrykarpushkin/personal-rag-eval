# Viktor Volkov

Coach of the Kalamaja Runners club, Tuesday and Thursday group sessions.

Ex-middle-distance runner, now coaches about fifteen of us. Tuesday is intervals or hills at Kadriorg, Thursday is tempo along the seafront. He speaks Russian when the English runs out, which suits me fine.

## Things he said
- "Marathon is built in the boring weeks"
- Cut the interval volume once the long runs pass 30 km
- Do not race the club 10k before the taper; use it as a hard training day

## Practical
- Meets at the lamppost by the Kalamaja park, 18:30
- He keeps everyone's paces in a sheet and updates after sessions
- Asked me to send my weekly plan on Sundays
