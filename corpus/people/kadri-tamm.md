# Kadri Tamm

Accountant; handles quarterly VAT and the annual income declaration for the sole-proprietor (FIE) setup. Small office in Telliskivi, answers email fast, prefers numbers in a spreadsheet over a long story.

Recommended by Hanna Kuusk, who used her for a side gig back when we both worked at the same company.

## How we work
- I send quarterly figures by the 10th of the month after the quarter closes.
- She files VAT (käibemaks) and tells me what to set aside.
- Annual declaration: she wants the full year's invoice list and receipts by early spring.
- Since August she has direct read access to my invoicing, which saves both of us a lot of forwarding.

## Open questions for her
- What changes in my tax situation when I move to Portugal, and when does Estonian residency effectively end?
- Whether the FIE stays alive or I switch to an OÜ. She is not sold on an OÜ for my income level.
