# Priya Raman

Product manager at Kestrel Freight, my day-to-day contact for Harbor. Kestrel is the client; Priya owns the Harbor roadmap on their side and talks to the dispatchers every day.

## Working with her
- Weekly sync, Tuesdays at 11:00 (video). She puts the agenda in the invite and mails a summary within the hour.
- Decides scope and priorities. Tomasz decides architecture. I try not to confuse the two.
- Likes to see anything before it goes to Tomasz.
- Writes clear, short emails. Replies fast in the daytime.
- Dislikes surprises more than delays. Tell her early when something slips.

## What she cares about
- Dispatchers actually using the tool. Her metric is Lena asking for the next feature.
- No downtime during Kestrel's peak hours (mornings).
- Honest estimates.
