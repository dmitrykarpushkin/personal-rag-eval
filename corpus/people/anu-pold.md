# Anu Põld

Runs the shared ceramics studio Savi in Telliskivi. Potter for fifteen years, trained in Tartu, thrown more mugs than anyone I know.

## What she is like
- Direct, funny, quick to correct a wobbling pot
- Very strict about wax resist on feet and about writing down every firing
- Likes to explain why a glaze behaves as it does, not just what to do

## Working with her
- She loads and unloads the kilns; members book shelf space
- She suggested the celadon thickness tests and the habit of numbering tiles
- Organises the weekend table at the Telliskivi market for members
- Hosts an evening open studio every month
