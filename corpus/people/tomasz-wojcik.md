# Tomasz Wójcik

Tomasz Wójcik, CTO of Kestrel Freight, signs off on Harbor architecture decisions. He is the person above Priya on anything technical: hosting, auth, data retention, third-party APIs.

## How he works
- Prefers short written proposals (one page, options, recommendation) over long calls. If it is longer than a page he stops reading.
- Replies to email within a day, usually late evening.
- Cares about: no vendor lock-in, being able to run the thing on a single box if needed, and not having his ops team paged at night.
- Not a fan of "clever" frontend tooling, but he accepted the stack once Priya showed him the prototype.
- Wants a real demo, not slides. The big one is in December.

## Things he has asked for so far
- A written note on where Harbor runs and who can get into it (see the infra log from May).
- Webhook-based tracking ingestion rather than polling carriers, eventually.
- Weekly one-line status mail, Fridays.

## Notes for myself
- Always send him the proposal via Priya first; Priya likes to see it before it goes up.
- He signed off on the hosting plan in the architecture review in June.
- Do not surprise him with scope changes in a meeting. Mail first, then discuss.
