# Mart Rebane

Landlord of the Kalamaja flat (Vabriku street). Retired engineer, lives in Nõmme, drops by about twice a year and always brings a jar of something pickled.

- Pays: rent goes to him by bank transfer on the 3rd; he replies to email within a day, ignores messages.
- Rules: no electrical work of any kind, no drilling into the load-bearing wall in the hallway, shelves are fine.
- Likes: being told early when something breaks. Hates: surprises.
- The radiator in the bedroom was bled in May, he sent a plumber the same week.
- He knows I am planning to leave for Lisbon, the notice period is something to check in the contract before I give it.
