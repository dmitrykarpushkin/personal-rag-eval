# Call with Hanna, 18 August

She called to pitch a role at her Berlin startup: lead product engineer on a tracking and customs platform for freight forwarders. Full-time employment, hybrid, relocation help offered.

## What she said
- The team is about twenty people, Series A done, looking for someone who "has already built dashboards that logistics people actually use". She thinks Harbor is exactly that story.
- Salary range was left for a second call, she did not want to anchor too early.
- Start in the new year at the earliest, so the timing is oddly compatible with my plans, just not the country.

## My side
- Flattered, genuinely. Told her I am committed to the Lisbon plan and to keeping my independence.
- Asked if remote from Portugal was an option. She said not for the first year.
- Agreed she can send me the full description so I can think about it properly.

## After

- Mention it to Oskar next time and see what he says.
