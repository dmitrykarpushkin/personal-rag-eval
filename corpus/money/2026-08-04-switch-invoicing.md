# Invoicing switch, 4 August

Switched invoicing from Invoice Ninja to Merit Aktiva because Kadri can access it directly. No more exporting PDFs and CSVs to her at the end of each quarter, she just logs in.

## Why now
- The self-hosted instance needed another update and I did not want to babysit it during the holiday month.
- Kadri asked twice whether she could see the invoice list live.
- Merit handles e-invoices to Estonian companies, which Kestrel Freight OÜ prefers.

## Steps
1. Create company in Merit, add VAT settings (Kadri checked them).
2. Import customer list: Kestrel Freight OÜ and the Glaze wholesale customers.
3. Recreate the invoice template with the same numbering scheme so the sequence does not break.
4. Keep the old instance read-only until the end of the year, then shut it down.

## Notes
- Past invoices stay where they are, I only moved the customer data.
- Cost is a few euros a month, fine.
- Remember to tell Priya that the sender address on the PDFs changes.
