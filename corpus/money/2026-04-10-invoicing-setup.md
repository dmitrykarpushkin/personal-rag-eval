# Invoicing setup, 10 April

Started the Harbor contract this month so I need proper invoicing before the first month ends. Set up Invoice Ninja (self-hosted) for invoices. I already have a small box that runs a few other tools, so it was an hour of work.

## Decisions
- Numbering: year prefix plus a running number, continuing through the whole year, no per-client prefix.
- Currency EUR, payment terms 14 days, bank details from the LHV account for now.
- Customers: Kestrel Freight OÜ is the only one so far; Glaze orders are paid at checkout so they do not need invoices unless a business asks.
- Email template in plain English, short, with the hours breakdown in the body as well as the PDF.
