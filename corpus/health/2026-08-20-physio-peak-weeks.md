# Physio, last pre-taper visit

Liis worked on the calves and hip flexors, which were tight after the 32 km run. She used some dry needling on the right calf; it hurts but it works.

Advice for the taper:
- Cut strength work to one light session
- Keep the foam roller going
- Sleep, sleep, sleep
- Do not try anything new on race week, not even a new massage

I left feeling lighter. The knee has not complained since the end of June.
