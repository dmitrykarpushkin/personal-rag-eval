# First chat with Liis

Booked mostly as prevention, since the volume jumped. Liis watched me walk and squat, checked ankle mobility and hip strength.

Findings:
- Left hip is stronger than the right; the right side drops a little on single-leg squats
- Calves tight, ankle mobility okay
- Nothing hurts yet except a faint knee pinch after long runs

She gave me three exercises: single-leg bridges, side planks and calf raises with a slow lowering phase. Told me to come back at once if the knee starts stinging on the outside.

I should have done this in April. Viktor has been saying that for weeks.
