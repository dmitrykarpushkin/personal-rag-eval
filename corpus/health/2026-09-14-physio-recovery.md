# Physio after the marathon

Recovery visit. Liis massaged the calves, which were still sore a week after the race, and checked the right knee, which had behaved perfectly the whole time.

- Calves recovering, normal for the effort
- Gentle running allowed again as soon as I feel like it
- Hip routine continues once or twice a week
- A bit of tightness in the left hamstring, probably compensating

She said the cramps were most likely a mix of pace, heat and not enough salt, matching my own guess. I told her about the spring marathon idea. She laughed and said "come back in six months."
