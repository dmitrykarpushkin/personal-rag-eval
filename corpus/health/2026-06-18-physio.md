# Physio with Liis

Went to Liis after the right knee started stinging on the outside around km 8 of Tuesday's tempo run. She tested hip abduction, did a Ober test, pressed along the outside of the thigh and found the tight spots quickly.

Liis says it's iliotibial band syndrome on the right knee; two weeks of reduced mileage, foam roller, hip strength work.

Plan:
- Cut weekly distance by roughly a third, no hills, no intervals
- Foam roller every evening, five minutes each side
- Clamshells and side planks, three sets, every other day
- Cycling is allowed if it does not sting
- Back to see her in ten days

Annoyed, but it caught early. I messaged Viktor so he could adjust the Tuesday sessions.
