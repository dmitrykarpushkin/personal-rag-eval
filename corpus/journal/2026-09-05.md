Day before the race. Picked up the bib in the afternoon, the expo was smaller than I thought but the queue was quick. Pasta at home, early night planned.

Legs feel fine, no twinge in the right knee since the physio weeks. The forecast says cool and a light wind, which is about perfect. Predicting 3:55 if the weather holds. If the first half goes out too fast I will blow up around km 32, so the plan is 5:35 per km early and then see.

John is coming up from Tartu tonight and will pace me for a stretch in the middle. Told him not to talk to me unless I look like I am dying.

Laid out: shoes, gels (four), the club vest, the salt tablets, safety pins. Phone stays in the armband.

Slightly nervous in a good way. Writing this down so tomorrow I can see how wrong I was.
