Lisbon, day three. The hills are steeper than my map suggested. Walked everywhere, ate too many pastries, sat in a cafe on a corner and watched the light on the buildings change.

The flats I saw were different from the photos: smaller, darker, with better balconies. Rui explained what a fair rent looks like for each area without trying to sell me anything. I like that.

My feet hurt. My head is full. I want to live here.
