# Passport renewal

Renew Estonian passport before Lisbon trip, due 2026-10-12, open.

- Book a time at the police and border guard service point (they release slots in the morning).
- Bring one photo that meets the requirements, the old passport, and the state fee receipt.
- Check expiry: the visa application will want plenty of validity left.
- The new passport is needed for the visa file, so do not leave it until the move.
