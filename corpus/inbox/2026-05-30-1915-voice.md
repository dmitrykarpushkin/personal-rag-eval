Memo after packaging test. Mug in a box with paper stuffing survived being dropped from table height, mug in a thin envelope did not. So: a sturdy small box and crinkle paper, a printed thank-you card, and a short note about care. Weigh the packed mug and write down the postage tiers. Do not forget stickers.
