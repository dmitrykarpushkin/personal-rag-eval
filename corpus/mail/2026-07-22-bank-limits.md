> We have updated our terms of service for online banking. Daily transfer limits remain unchanged unless you choose otherwise in the settings.

I looked at the settings. The default limit is fine for normal use, but a bigger transfer like a flat deposit will need me to raise it in advance and confirm with a code on the phone.

Add to Lisbon checklist:
- ask the bank (and the other account) how long raising a limit takes
- keep the euro account active, since Portuguese landlords usually want a transfer from an IBAN, not cash
