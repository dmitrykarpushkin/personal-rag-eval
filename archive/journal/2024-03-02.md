First proper run of the year over 10 km. 10.4, to be exact, down to the Noblessner marina and back. Legs heavy, but the sun was out and the bay was half frozen, grey plates of ice knocking against each other.

I keep thinking about entering a half marathon in the autumn. Not decided. Writing it here so that future me can see I thought about it in March.
