Glögg evening with friends from university, six of us in a tiny kitchen. John Kask came up from Tartu and told stories about customers who bring bikes in a box with the wheels in a bag. He sold one that day, he said, to a man who wanted "something fast but not too fast".

Walked home at one. Steam from the sea, minus six.
