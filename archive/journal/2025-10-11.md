Day three in Lisbon. Tram 28 was packed, so I walked. Everything is hills and tiles and laundry hung out of windows. Pastéis de nata at a place where the counter guy knew exactly who was a tourist and charged me nothing extra.

I sat on a miradouro with a glass of white wine and a notebook and wrote one line: "Could I live here?" Then crossed it out and wrote it again.
