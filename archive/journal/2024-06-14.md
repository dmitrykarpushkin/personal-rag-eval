Mývatn. The smell of sulphur, steam coming out of the ground like the planet is slightly overcooked. Soaked in the hot springs at midnight with bright daylight overhead. A woman next to me from Canada said it was her fifth ring road. I said it was my first and she said "you will be back", with total confidence.

Photos do not do any of this justice. I stopped taking them around noon.
