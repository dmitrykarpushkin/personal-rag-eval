Day three. Vik, black sand, waves that look like they mean it. Drove past waterfalls so casually that I stopped stopping. Skógafoss I did stop for, and wore the wrong jacket. Soaked.

The car smells of wet wool. Slept in a guesthouse where the owner served skyr at breakfast and explained how to pronounce everything, patiently, twice.
