Narva. Slept in my old room under the poster I never took down. Mum made pelmeni with Elin, I was allowed to pinch the edges, which was apparently the quality-control step.

Walked along the river to the fortress with Elin. Across the water you can see Ivangorod, and every time I am a bit stunned by how close it is. Elin talked about maybe going part-time at the hospital. She is tired. I said I would help however I can, and she said "just visit", which is the right answer.

Bus back at six, fell asleep against the window.
