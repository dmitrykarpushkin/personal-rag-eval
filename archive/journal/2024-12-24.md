Christmas Eve in Narva. Mum cooked far too much, Elin came straight off a shift and fell asleep for an hour on the sofa in her coat. We opened presents quietly. I gave mum a good scarf, she gave me wool socks (again) and a small notebook with a pressed flower in it.

The old apartment smells like cinnamon and radiators. I want to stay longer than two days next year.
