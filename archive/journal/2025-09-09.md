First class. We learned to wedge clay and center it. I could not center. At all. It wobbled like a drunk. Anu said that nearly everybody fails the first evening and then walked behind me, put her hands over mine and the clay obeyed. Embarrassing and magical.

My hands smell of wet earth and I like it.
