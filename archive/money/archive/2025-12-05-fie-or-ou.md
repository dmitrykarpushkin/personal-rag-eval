# FIE or OÜ, my thinking

Comparison, as I understand it:

FIE: simple registration, no minimum capital, personal liability, accounting is lighter. Income taxed as mine.
OÜ: separate legal entity, share capital, more reporting, profit tax only when distributed. Makes sense when income is bigger and I want to leave money inside.

For year one: FIE. Revisit if I earn well beyond expectations. Questions for Kadri: is there a point where OÜ clearly wins, what about moving abroad later (a vague question, not asking about specific places yet).
