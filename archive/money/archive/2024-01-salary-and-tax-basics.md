# What my payslip says

Written for myself because I never understood the payslip.

Gross: €4,100. Deductions: income tax 20% on the taxable part, 1.6% unemployment insurance, 2% funded pension. Net lands around €3,050. The employer adds social tax on top, I do not see it.

Estonian system: the tax-free allowance applies monthly, I just do not notice it. Good to know for a freelance future: no one will do this for me.
