# Check-in with Kadri

30 minutes. We went through January and February. Everything is in order. Her tips:
- keep a log of home-office costs, you can claim a proportion
- one invoice template, numbered consistently
- do not buy gear in December just to save tax, the discount is smaller than it looks
- send documents monthly, not quarterly, the folder gets messy quickly

She also said that if I ever think about moving abroad I should tell her early, because it changes the tax residence question. I said it is just an idea. She said, "those are usually the ones."
