# Strava paid

Took the paid plan at last, €59.99/year, because the Kalamaja Runners group posts the weekly sessions there and the training plan feature is only in the paid tier. John Kask uses it too so we can follow each other's runs. The route maps are nice, the rest I could do without. Renews at the end of October each year.
