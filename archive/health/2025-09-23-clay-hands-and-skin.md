Skin on the right hand is cracked at the thumb after three clay evenings. The GP's advice from the spring, "if something does not heal in a week, show it", feels useful. For now: thick cream at night, cotton gloves on top, short nails. A pharmacist recommended a barrier cream for craftspeople, the sort for hairdressers. It works.

Does clay dust matter for lungs? I looked it up: in the studio, wet cleaning only, never dry sweeping, and a mask when mixing glaze powders. Anu does that already.
