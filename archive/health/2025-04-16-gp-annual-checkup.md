# GP, April

Annual visit. Blood pressure normal, pulse 54 (the family doctor was visibly pleased), lungs and heart fine. Joined a running club, I told her. She said good, "social running is the one with the best adherence." I took that as permission.

Asked about vitamin D and I admitted I forgot again. She told me to put the tablet next to the coffee machine. Also recommended a few strength exercises for the hips since I spend a lot of time sitting.

Everything else: fine. Walk out into the spring sun with the feeling of having passed a test I did not know I was taking.
