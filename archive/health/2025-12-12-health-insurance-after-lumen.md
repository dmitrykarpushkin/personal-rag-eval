# What happens to my health cover after Lumen

Facts from Kadri and the Estonian health fund page:
- The Estonian health insurance (Haigekassa) continues while I work, and as a sole trader I need to arrange my own contribution through the tax settings. Kadri will set it up in the FIE planning
- The extra private cover from Lumen (dental and a few specialist visits) ends with my last day. I will not replace it for now
- Keep the family doctor: no change
- Dentist and eye visits: I have done both for the year, good timing
