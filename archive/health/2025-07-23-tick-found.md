Found a tick behind my knee after the week in the countryside. Pulled it out with tweezers, straight, slowly, the way the pharmacist showed me. Took a photo, noted the date and the spot, and disinfected.

Since I had the tick booster last year I am in the safe group for encephalitis. Borrelia is another story, so I will watch the spot for a red ring over the next weeks and see a doctor if it appears.

Note to self: long socks in the forest and a check every evening. Elin said "you are an idiot who runs in forests." I replied with a heart.
