# Eye exam with Dr. Mänd

Long visit, almost an hour. They did drops for the pressure and a few machines that flashed lights at me, then a regular chart test.

Findings:
- Right eye got a bit worse, left almost the same. A small astigmatism shows up on the right
- Optic nerves and retina: fine. He showed me the picture of the back of my eye on the screen, lovely, like a red planet
- Pressure normal
- The halos at night are from the prescription mismatch, not from anything scary

New glasses prescribed. He said running in the dark is fine, as long as I can see; "you were running half-blind for a year, now you have a chance to see your own city." I laughed.

Picked frames at the shop next door: a slim dark pair, light. Ready in a couple of days. He told me to come back if anything changes suddenly (flashing lights, curtain across the vision).
