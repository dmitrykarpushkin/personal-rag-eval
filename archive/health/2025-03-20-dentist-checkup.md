# Dentist check-up, March 2025

Dr. Saar again. Clean, polish, an X-ray of the back teeth. All good: no new cavities, last year's filling holding up. She said my flossing is "visibly improved", which I accept as the best review of my life.

Small thing: a slight chip on the edge of an upper front tooth. She smoothed it down in two minutes. I think it happened on a bun from Telliskivi Market. She laughed.

I came straight from a 10 km run and she could probably smell it. She did not comment.

Left with a new soft toothbrush and the feeling of a person with their life in order.
