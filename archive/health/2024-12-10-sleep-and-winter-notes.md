Dark by half past three, I wake up tired. Sleep tracking says about six hours and ten minutes on a typical night, which is less than I want. Plan:

- Screens off by 22:30
- Bedroom window cracked, thick duvet
- Melatonin? No. Talk to GP before any supplements
- A walk at noon in daylight on workdays

Since I started with the evening walk after dinner the sleep got a little better: 6:40 on average for the last week. Also lighter mornings in my head. It is not medical advice, just a log for April, when the GP will ask me again.
