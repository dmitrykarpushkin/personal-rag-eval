# Tight left calf

Four weeks to the half and the left calf has been tight for three days after the Thursday session. No sharp pain, just a hard knot in the lower half. What I am doing:

- Foam roller 5 minutes morning and evening
- Calf stretches on the stairs, 3 x 30 s
- Eccentric heel drops, 3 x 15 every other day (heard about them from a runner on Reddit)
- Sleeping with a pillow under the legs, which seems like a myth but feels nice

If it is not better in a week or hurts when I walk, I go to the doctor. Cut Thursday's workout from 8 x 1 min to 5 x 1 min.
