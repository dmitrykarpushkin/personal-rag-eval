# Day 1, Tallinn to Keflavik

Long travel day. The change was fine, the second flight was half empty. Landed late and it was still bright at midnight, which my body does not believe. Picked up the car. The man at the desk said, "wind today is not bad", and then I saw the wind. Airport hotel for the first night.

Triin took the first photo of the trip: me holding the car door with both hands. Instant meme.
