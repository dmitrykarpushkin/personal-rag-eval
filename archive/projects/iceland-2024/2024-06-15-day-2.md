# Day 2, Reykjavik to Hella

Slept until nine, first time in months. Breakfast, then Golden Circle: Þingvellir (walked the rift, cold), Geysir (waited, then it went off while I was looking at my phone), Gullfoss. Everything is bigger than the photos.

Hella farm guesthouse tonight, hot tub on the porch. Sat in it at 23:00 in full daylight. Spent €19 on a lamb soup for dinner, it was worth it. Grocery run for lunch supplies.
