# What I would do differently

- Two nights in Höfn/east instead of one, the fjords deserve more time.
- Not bother with the Golden Circle on day 2, everyone does it, it was crowded, the south coast was better.
- Book the whale trip earlier, ours was nearly full.
- Bring a decent camera lens or stop pretending.
- Keep a slow day in the middle. That was right.

Biggest feeling: I do my best thinking when I am driving through nowhere. Come back to this when I decide things in autumn.
