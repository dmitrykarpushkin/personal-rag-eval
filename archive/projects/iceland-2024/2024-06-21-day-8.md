# Day 8, Húsavík and Akureyri

Whale watching in the morning. We saw two humpbacks, one came up right by the boat, and I forgot to take any photos because I was staring. The old wooden boat smelled like tea and wool blankets. €79 each, expensive, absolutely worth it.

Then Akureyri: botanical garden, ice cream (yes, even here), the apartment with a washing machine. Did laundry. Felt very adult.
