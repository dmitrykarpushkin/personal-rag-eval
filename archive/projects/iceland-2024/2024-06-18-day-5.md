# Day 5, Höfn to Seyðisfjörður

East fjords all day, winding roads, the light turning every colour. The pass down to Seyðisfjörður is steep and I may have shut my eyes briefly. The town is a postcard: wooden houses, the blue church, rainbow street.

The old house has creaky stairs as promised and we did get the sea side. Fog came in at ten and swallowed the fjord.
