# Day 10, back to Reykjavik

Morning walk on the coast, then the drive back around the fjord to Reykjavik. Reykjavik for the afternoon: Hallgrímskirkja, a hot dog from the famous stand, souvenir shopping for Elin (a wool hat), a mug for me from a small ceramics shop. I kept turning it over in my hands, looking at the glaze. Added "try pottery" to the idea list again.

Returned the car with the dents we already had. Last night at the airport hotel.
