# Reading about weather

Lots of reading tonight. Takeaways:
- Wind is the real hazard, car doors can be ripped off. Park facing into the wind and hold the door with both hands.
- Gravel roads can close for mud in the east in early summer.
- Temperatures 6-14 °C is normal in June. Layers.
- Check the road site and the weather site every morning. Triin knows this already, she has been reading the same pages.

Also the sheep. Sheep on the road, and nobody mentions how many. Slow down.
