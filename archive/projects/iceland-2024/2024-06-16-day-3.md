# Day 3, South coast to Vík

Seljalandsfoss (walked behind the fall, wet), Skógafoss (climbed the stairs, ~500 steps, wonderful), then the black beach at Reynisfjara. Sneaker waves, we kept well back after the sign scared us.

Vík guesthouse is tiny and the shared bathroom is fine. Dinner was sandwiches and crisps on the beach. Triin took 140 photos of puffins that I think are 4 puffins.
