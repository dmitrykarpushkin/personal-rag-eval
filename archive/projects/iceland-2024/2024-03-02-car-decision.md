# Car: small 4x4, not a camper

Compared three options:
- Camper van: romantic, but nights are cold even in June and a van uses more fuel. Also none of the cheap ones were free for our dates.
- Compact car: cheapest, but the gravel to Seyðisfjörður and some F-road-adjacent bits make me nervous, plus gravel insurance.
- Small 4x4 (the Dacia Duster size): about 35% more than the compact, still less than the camper.

Decision: small 4x4 with full gravel and sand/ash insurance. Pick-up at the airport. Triin has driven gravel in Estonia plenty, fine.

Booked for ten days, about €830 total with insurance, split. Fuel is extra and I assume it's expensive.
