# Day 9, north to Snæfellsnes

The long driving day, about 5.5 hours with stops. Triin drove most of it. A few sheep standoffs. The Snæfellsnes peninsula in late light, the church at Búðir, the black beach. Kirkjufell from the car park, and the shot everyone takes, we took it too.

The farm stay has horses next door. We watched them for a long time. Dinner at the farm, fish of the day.
