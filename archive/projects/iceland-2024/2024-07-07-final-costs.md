# Final costs, per person

Went over my April estimate. Actuals:

- flights €310
- car with insurance €415
- guesthouses €535
- fuel €196 (it is as expensive as everyone said)
- food €402 (the langoustine soup effect)
- activities €215 (whales were the big one)
- misc €88

Total about €2,160. Under the €2,400 ceiling, over my estimate by roughly €90. Honestly very good for ten days in one of the most expensive countries in Europe.
