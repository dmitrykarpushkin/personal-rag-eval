# Day 4, Vík to Höfn via the lagoon

Long drive through lava fields covered in moss. Stopped at Fjaðrárgljúfur canyon, then Skaftafell for a short glacier walk (no crampons, just the viewpoint trail). The glacier lagoon was the thing I will remember, icebergs drifting out to sea, seals.

Höfn hostel, private room. Had langoustine soup, the one non-supermarket splurge of the day, €27.
