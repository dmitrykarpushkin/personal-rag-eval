# Route draft v1

Counter-clockwise from Reykjavik. Rough driving per day, from the maps app, so probably optimistic:

1. Keflavik, Reykjavik (short day, arrive late)
2. Golden Circle, then Hella area (~3h)
3. South coast: Seljalandsfoss, Skógafoss, Vík (~3h)
4. Vík to Skaftafell, Jökulsárlón (~3.5h)
5. Höfn, East fjords, Seyðisfjörður (~4h)
6. Egilsstaðir to Mývatn via Dettifoss (~3.5h)
7. Mývatn, Húsavík (whales?)
8. Akureyri, Siglufjörður side trip
9. Skagafjörður, Snæfellsnes (~5h, long)
10. Snæfellsnes back to Reykjavik, fly out next morning

Total about 1,700 km with detours. Too much? Triin says fine, the road is empty. I'd like one slow day somewhere, probably Mývatn. Will revisit after I see guesthouse availability.
