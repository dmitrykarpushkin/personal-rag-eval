# Iceland, finally

Triin messaged tonight asking if I still wanted to do Iceland "before we are 35". I said yes before I thought about it. Work has been heavy with the Beacon launch, and I want something with long empty roads and no meetings.

Questions to settle this month:
- June or August? June has the light, August has the better chance of warm weather and the highlands open (we are not doing highlands anyway).
- Camper or guesthouses? I vote guesthouses, I like a real bed and a shower.
- How many days. Ten feels right, two weeks is too much leave.

Asked Oskar if the second half of June is fine for leave. He said just put it in the calendar and stop asking permission.
