# Guesthouses

Booked, nine nights in total (night 1 we are at the airport hotel by Keflavik, flights come late):

- Hella area: farm guesthouse, breakfast included, hot tub (!)
- Vík: small guesthouse near the church, shared bathroom, cheapest of the trip
- Skaftafell / Kirkjubæjarklaustur: cottage, kitchen
- Höfn: hostel private room
- Seyðisfjörður: old house with creaky stairs, reviews all say "ask for the sea side"
- Mývatn: guesthouse, two nights
- Akureyri: apartment
- Snæfellsnes: farm stay

Everything has free cancellation until early June. The Höfn one was the only place nearly sold out, which tells me we are not early enough. Total for stays so far €1,070 for two rooms-worth, i.e. about €535 each. That is the biggest line in the budget.
