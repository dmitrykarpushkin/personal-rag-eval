# Day 6, to Mývatn

Left late because of fog. Drove over the high plateau to Dettifoss, wind trying to push me into the canyon, so photos from a safe distance. The Mývatn landscape is Mars: steaming vents, mud pots, sulphur smell.

Geothermal baths in the evening, milky blue water, 38 degrees, midges in the car park. Bought repellent after the first bite.
