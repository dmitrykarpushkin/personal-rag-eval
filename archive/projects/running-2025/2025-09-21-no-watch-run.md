Ran 12 km without the watch, only a phone in my pocket as a camera. The seafront in September: light low and gold, a heron on a concrete block, two men fishing in thick jumpers.

I came home and checked afterwards. 6:08/km, almost exactly what I would have guessed. That is what a season of tempo runs does: the body knows the pace without looking.

I should do this more often. The watch is a very good servant and a bad boss.
