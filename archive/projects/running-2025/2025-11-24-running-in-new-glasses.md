# Running with the new glasses

New glasses arrived Friday. Today was the first run with them: 8 km easy in the dark, and I could see every pothole, tram wire and Estonian drunk. It is like a patch has been lifted from the screen.

I wear them on the run for now, but they fog up when I am warm and stop for a minute at traffic lights. Might try a sports band or contacts later. For now an old cap and a thin band stops them sliding.

The world has edges again.
