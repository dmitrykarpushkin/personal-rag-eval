# 2025 in running

Distance: about 1,650 km. More than last year by a quarter.

Moments I want to remember:
- Joining Kalamaja Runners in April, the tea after the first Tuesday
- Tartu half in 1:54:12 with John at km 15 holding a sign
- 5k time trial, 24:06
- Cheering with a cowbell at km 30 and wanting, secretly, to be in the race
- A morning run along the river in Lisbon

Things that went less well: a skipped summer long run or three, too many double-sugar coffees, a stubborn calf in October.

Next year: I leave Lumen and go freelance, so the days will be mine. Running will have to fit with clients, not with office hours. I want sub-1:50 in a half and something, maybe, longer. Ask me in spring.
