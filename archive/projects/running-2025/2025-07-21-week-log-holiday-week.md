# Week 30 (14-20 Jul): holiday week

A week off from work, spent in Estonian countryside with friends: a lake, a sauna, a small house with a very slow internet connection.

Runs: 5 km on forest roads, 8 km with a long hill, 12 km on Sunday to a village shop and back (6:10, buying ice cream was part of the plan).

25 km, but great quality. No watch on three of them. I felt like I used to as a kid: running because the road was there.
