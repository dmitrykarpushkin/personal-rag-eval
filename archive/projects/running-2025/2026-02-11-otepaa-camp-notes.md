# Otepää camp: what I learned

- Running 70 minutes on soft snow is equal to 85 minutes on the road. Calves cry
- Hills at easy effort are free strength. We did a hill loop four times and I got stronger just by being tired
- The sauna rule: no talk about work, no phones, only about food
- Viktor watches everyone's form, even when he pretends to look at his watch. Mine: "less bounce".
- The group is warm; a few of them have been running together for ten years and still tease each other about 2015.

I want to be like that in ten years: tired, slow, laughing in a sauna with people I like.
