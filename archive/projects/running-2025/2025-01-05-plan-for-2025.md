# 2025: what I want from running

Concrete:
- Tartu half marathon in May, with John as the reason to go. Target under 1:55
- Join a club. Kalamaja Runners meets near me on Tuesdays and Thursdays, coach is Viktor Volkov. I have been reading their page for a month and not writing to them
- Stay injury-free: add strength 2x a week (hip bridges, single-leg stuff, calves)
- Cancel the gym membership if I am not using it by March. I go to the gym three times a year and each time I pay for the other eleven months

Not goals: a marathon. Not this year. Maybe not ever.
