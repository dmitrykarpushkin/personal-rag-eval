# Back after the flu (20-26 Jan)

Doctor said OK on Friday. Sat 4 km very easy, Sun 6 km. That is all.

It felt like the first run of my life: heavy legs, tight chest, stupid grin. Eased in, kept the heart rate low on purpose.

Lost about two and a half weeks. Fitness has probably dropped but I tell myself it will come back faster than it left. Moving the Tartu half plan down by one week: build starts in February, not January.
