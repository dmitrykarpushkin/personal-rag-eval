Midsummer morning, 6 km on the empty seafront. The city was so quiet I could hear my own breathing and a distant radio. Passed two people who were coming home from a party in last night's clothes; they cheered. A perfect, silly run.

Bonfire yesterday was nice: someone brought a guitar and not a single person could play it, which was fine. Jumped over the fire once, did not set anything on fire.

Mood: light. Slept four hours and felt great.
