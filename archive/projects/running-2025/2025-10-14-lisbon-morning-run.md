# A run in Lisbon

Ran 6 km along the river, from Cais do Sodré toward Belém and back, at seven in the morning when it was already 17 degrees. I had noticed the joggers on Sunday and promised myself I would try it. The light on the water was gold and the 25 de Abril bridge hummed overhead.

Hills are no joke: I tried one street in Alfama and walked the last bit. Climbing a cobbled hill in sneakers is a skill I do not have.

Stopped at a kiosk for a coffee with milk and a pastel de nata. The woman said something friendly in Portuguese and I answered in English, embarrassed.

Everything in this city seems a little too good for October. Ate far too much fish.
