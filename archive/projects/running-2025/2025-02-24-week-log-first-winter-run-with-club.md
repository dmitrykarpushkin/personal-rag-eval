# Week 8 (17-23 Feb)

Tue 7 km, Thu 8 km, Sat 10 km, Sun 14 km.

39 km. Saturday I joined the winter easy run of Kalamaja Runners: eleven people, 10 km, almost no talking in the first 3 km and then suddenly everyone chatting. I ran slower than my comfortable pace and it was easier than running alone.

One of them, a guy named Joosep, said the real work happens on Tuesdays. Another, a woman with a very serious headlamp, said "ignore him, it is all for the coffee after." The coffee after was real.

I have stopped going to the gym completely. Told them I am cancelling.
