# Saturday night at John's

Got off the train at 18:30, John was at the station with the bike and a basket, as promised, and a hand-lettered sign "MARA, DEAD OR ALIVE". He rode alongside me while I walked the ten minutes to his flat, narrating the history of every bike shop in town.

Dinner: his pasta, a very big pot. A bit too much garlic for race eve but honestly no regrets. Talked about university, his shop (the winter was slow, spring is picking up), my plans. He asked whether I will ever do a marathon and I said "no" in a voice that made him smile.

In bed by 22:30 on the couch. Alarm 06:30. Shoes by the door. I am calmer than last September, which probably means something good.
