# Spectator day

Tallinn Marathon day. Eight of us from Kalamaja Runners stood at km 30 near the Pirita promenade with cowbells, a bedsheet banner ("VIKTOR SAYS KEEP GOING") and a thermos of sweet tea for no one in particular.

Watched Joosep go by in 3:42, jaw set, giving us a thumbs up with one finger. Katrin ran past in 3:28, a clear PB, she waved. Several others walked, grinned or cried at us.

I shouted until I had no voice left. Seeing the marathon from the side of the road is very different. The faces change between km 28 and km 32.

Not tempted, I told myself, for approximately three hours. Then someone asked "next year?" and I said "no" far too quickly.
