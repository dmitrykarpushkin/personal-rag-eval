# Run in Narva (Easter Sunday)

Ran 12 km along the Narva river promenade on Sunday morning, from the Hermann castle side toward the fortress and back. The Russian castle across the water looked very close. Cool, fresh air and almost nobody out.

Elin joined the first 3 km on her bike, in a hospital hoodie. She said: "You are going faster than last time." I said "I am going slower than I want." She said "that is what faster people say."

Mum had pelmeni and tea ready. I ate eleven. That was the real long run.
