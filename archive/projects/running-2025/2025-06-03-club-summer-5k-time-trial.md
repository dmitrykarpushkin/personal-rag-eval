# 5k time trial with the club

Tuesday evening, the stadium track, a timer and a very serious Viktor.

**5 km in 24:06.** I thought I could do 24:30, so happy. Splits: 4:50, 4:47, 4:50, 4:53, 4:46. Last lap I was passing a guy who definitely hated me for it.

Katrin ran 22:50 and did not seem tired. I asked how. She said "I just do it more often than you." Fair.

Viktor's note on my form: "You overstride when tired. Quicker steps." I will add short cadence drills on Thursdays.
