# Otepää camp, day 1

Arrived at noon, minus 11, sunshine and trees heavy with frost. A wooden guesthouse, a shared sauna, twenty runners and one very enthusiastic coach.

First session: 10 km in the forest on packed snow paths, easy, with strides at the end. Then a long lunch, then stretching in the corridor.

Viktor's rule: "No heroics. This week is about getting stronger, not faster." Katrin said "of course" and then ran the whole thing at her quick pace.

At dinner someone asked what my next race was. I said "I don't know". Joosep said "that is not an answer". Okay, it is a question I should answer.
