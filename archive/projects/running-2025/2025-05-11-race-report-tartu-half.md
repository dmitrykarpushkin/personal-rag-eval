# Race report: Tartu half marathon

**Official time: 1:54:12.** Nearly five minutes faster than last September, and under my 1:55 target.

Weather: 13 degrees, bright sun, light wind. The river looked like a postcard.

I started in the 1:55 group and tried to keep it relaxed. 5:27, 5:25, 5:26, almost metronome, the first 10 km at 54:20. Viktor had told me "do not be a hero before km 15." At km 15 John was standing in the middle of the road with a cardboard sign that said "МЛ, ПОБЕЖДАЙ!!" (my initials and a Russian "win!", in his awful handwriting). I shouted at him.

Last 5 km hurt. 5:30, 5:29, 5:33, 5:29, and the last 1.1 km at about 5:15 with everything I had.

Finish: 1:54:12, hands on knees, John shoved a cinnamon bun at me. We had soup at his place and I fell asleep on the sofa in the afternoon sun. Home by the evening train.

Viktor's message afterwards: "Good. Now rest."
