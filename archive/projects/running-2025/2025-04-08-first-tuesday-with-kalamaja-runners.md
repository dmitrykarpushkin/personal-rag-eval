# First official Tuesday with the club

18:30, near the Kalamaja park. Around twenty-five people in a loose group, bright jackets everywhere. Viktor Volkov: tall, calm, a stopwatch on a lanyard from before smartwatches existed. He asked what I ran and I said "1:58 in September last year." He nodded and said "so, 5:40. Run with Katrin's group."

The workout was 6 x 800 m on the stadium track near Kadriorg, 400 jog. I did them at 4:50-4:55. The last one was almost 4:45 because someone passed me and I could not help it.

Afterwards a few stayed for tea. Everyone was friendly, no one asked for an Instagram. I think I belong here. Next week: Thursday as well.
