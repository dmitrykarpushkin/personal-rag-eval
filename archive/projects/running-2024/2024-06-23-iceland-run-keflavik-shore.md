# Iceland run 4: Keflavik shore

Last run of the trip, from the airport hotel the evening before the flight. 6 km on the sea path outside Keflavik, lava on one side, grey Atlantic on the other, an old fishing boat on the grass. A few cyclists and one man with a metal detector.

Pace 5:50, and it felt easy, which was a pleasant surprise after ten days of driving, sitting and eating.

Total running on the trip: 4 runs, 30 km. Not impressive, but exactly what I planned and no injuries. The ankle held up on gravel. I did not wear out the old shoes as I thought I would.

Home tomorrow. The block continues on Monday.
