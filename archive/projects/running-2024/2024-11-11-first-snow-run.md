# First snow

Snow started during a 7 km run, fat flakes in the headlamp beam. Everyone in Kalamaja looked up at the same time like at a firework.

Pace was slow and I did not check it once. I slipped once near the tram stop but kept my feet. Home with wet socks and a stupid grin, hot shower, soup.

I should write this down: running in winter is not suffering, it is the part you get to keep for yourself.
