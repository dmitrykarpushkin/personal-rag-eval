# Running while travelling: Iceland

We leave on Friday the 14th for the ring road with Triin: a rental car, guesthouses, and a lot of hot dogs.

Running rules for the trip:
- Pack the older pair, keep the newer ones for the half
- Four runs maximum, none longer than 10 km
- Wind is the enemy. Check the wind direction and take the headwind first
- No heroics on hills or lava rock, sprained ankle would ruin the rest of the summer

I looked at the map for pretty options: the coast near Vík, the fjord in Seyðisfjörður, the path in Akureyri, the sea in Keflavik at midnight sun. I will log each one in a short note.
