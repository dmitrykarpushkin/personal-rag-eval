# 14 weeks to the half: the block

Race: Tallinn half marathon, Sunday 8 September. Goal: under 2:00, which is 5:41/km. A stretch goal in my head is 1:55 but I will not say it loud.

Rough shape of the week:
- Tue: easy 8 km with 6 strides
- Thu: workout (intervals, then tempo as the weeks go on)
- Sat: easy 6-8 km
- Sun: long run, building from 14 to 19 km, with a down week every fourth

I wrote this plan on paper first and then typed it up, which is probably the most "me" thing I have done this year. Printed copy goes on the fridge door.

First week: 34 km. Iceland will interrupt it for ten days, which I have decided is a feature.
