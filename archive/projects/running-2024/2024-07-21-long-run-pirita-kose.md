# Long run: Kalamaja to Pirita and back, 19 km

Left at 07:30 to beat the heat. Route: seafront past Kadriorg, onto the Pirita promenade, turn at the beach, then back along the same line. Two water stops, one at a kiosk that opened as I got there (the woman looked at me like I was a ghost).

- First 10 km: 6:03/km
- Last 9 km: 5:55/km. Negative split, which I did not plan.
- Fuel: gel at 8 and 14
- Finish: sweaty, hungry, euphoric

19 km is the longest run before the race. In training plan terms this is basically "I can do this." My legs felt a bit dead at 15-16 but picked up again.

Ate a huge breakfast: eggs, bread, cucumber and a coffee bigger than my head.
