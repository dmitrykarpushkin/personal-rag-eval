# Running in 2024: rough plan

I stopped running properly in October. Four months of walking to the tram and nothing else. Today I did 5 km along the Kalamaja seafront at -6 and my lungs hated me, but it felt good to be outside before noon.

What I want this year, in order of how much I care:
1. Run three times a week, every week, until spring. Not fast. Just regular.
2. Do one real race. A half marathon in September feels right: far enough away that I can't fake it, close enough to stay serious.
3. Stop doing every run at the same medium-hard pace.

I have never run more than 15 km. The half is 21.1. That gap is the whole project.

Gear: the grey shoes are on about 700 km and the left heel is collapsing. Need new ones by March. Also buy a proper hat, my ears turned into small red bricks today.
