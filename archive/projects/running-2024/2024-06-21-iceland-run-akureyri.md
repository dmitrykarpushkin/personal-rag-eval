# Iceland run 3: Akureyri

Evening run, after the whales, around the fjord path, 9 km, 6:02 pace. Felt the best I have felt all year. Midnight light, 12 degrees, no wind. I did not take any photos because I was too busy running.

Eating on this trip: hot dogs, skyr, lamb soup, and one langoustine soup that was worth all of it. My stomach has been surprisingly good.

Tomorrow we drive west to Snæfellsnes. Home on the 24th and then it is full-on half marathon season.
