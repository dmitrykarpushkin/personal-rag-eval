# Race report: Tallinn half marathon

**Official time: 1:58:40.** Under two hours! Position somewhere in the middle of the pack, I do not care.

Weather: 15 degrees, cloudy, a little wind on the bridge. Close to ideal.

Splits (from the watch, not official):
- 5 km: 28:30 (too fast by ten seconds, I was drunk on the crowd)
- 10 km: 56:10
- 15 km: 1:24:25
- 20 km: 1:52:50
- finish: 1:58:40

First half was smooth, almost boring. At km 14 my left calf twitched and I thought "not now". It stayed twitchy but quiet. At km 17 people started walking ahead of me and I passed about fifteen of them, which is the best feeling in sport. Last kilometre along the finish chute I cried a bit and a volunteer gave me a banana and said "good job".

Afterwards: lay on the grass near the finish, ate, drank, then walked home very slowly. Texted Elin the time. She replied "горжусь" with six exclamation marks.

Lesson: the training worked, so did the taper. Next goal: faster, but also enjoy it.
