# Iceland run 2: Seyðisfjörður

Slept badly because of the fog horn I hope I imagined. Ran 8 km out along the fjord road and back before breakfast, with the water flat as a plate on the left and mountains going straight up on the right. The blue church looked like a toy from the road.

Pace 6:15, totally relaxed. One car passed me in the whole run, and the driver waved like we knew each other. Then I had to stop and walk up the final hill to the guesthouse, creaky stairs and all.

Triin asked if I was always like this on holiday. I said no, usually worse.
