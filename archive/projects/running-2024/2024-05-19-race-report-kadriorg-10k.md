# Race report: Kadriorg 10k

**Official time: 52:41.** New PB by almost four minutes.

It was 9 degrees, cloudy and nearly windless, almost perfect. The park was full of people with numbers on their chests pretending they were not nervous.

- km 1-3: 5:12, 5:10, 5:09. I told myself I was holding back.
- km 4-6: 5:13 each. Comfortable-ish.
- km 7-8: this is where it started to hurt. 5:14, 5:16.
- km 9-10: gritted my teeth and picked off people. 5:08, 5:02.

Everything I ate before the race stayed down. Started too fast again by a few seconds but not enough to hurt. The crowd at the second lap was loud and I may have waved at a stranger's child.

Afterwards: sat on the grass for ten minutes, bought a coffee, called my sister. She said "ты сумасшедшая" and asked how fast. I was insufferable.

Takeaway for September: 5:12/km for 10k means I can probably hold 5:40 for 21k if training goes well.
