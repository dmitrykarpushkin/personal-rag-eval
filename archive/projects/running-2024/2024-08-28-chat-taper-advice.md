Chat with a friend from the running subreddit about the taper (copy for myself):

- Cut volume by about 20% in the first taper week, 40% in the second, then race week very light
- Keep some intensity, short and sharp (strides, 4 x 400 at race effort)
- Sleep is the real training in the last two weeks
- Nothing new on race day: food, shoes, socks, even the same breakfast
- If you get a cold sensation, ignore it and do not go for a "test run" at the last moment

I asked them if it is normal to feel more tired during the taper. "Yes, extremely, it is the body repairing." Okay then.
