# New shoes

Went to the running shop in Rotermann and got properly looked at on the treadmill. The guy filmed my feet from behind. Mild overpronation, nothing dramatic, he said, and he put me in a neutral shoe with a bit more cushion than I expected.

Tried four pairs. The blue ones felt like running on a pillow, the black ones like running on a table. Took the green-grey pair, which was somewhere in between. They are a normal daily trainer, nothing carbon.

Old shoes retired after about 700 km. I will keep them for walking in the rain.

Rule: new shoes get one short run before any long one. First test tomorrow, 5 km.
