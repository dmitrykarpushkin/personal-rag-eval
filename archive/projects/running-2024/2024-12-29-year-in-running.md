# 2024 in running

Total distance: about 1,310 km (watch says so, I don't fully trust it).

Highlights:
- Kadriorg 10k in May, 52:41, big PB
- Iceland runs in June, none longer than 9 km, all of them lovely
- Tallinn half in September, 1:58:40

Lowlights:
- Hip tightness and a bad calf week in August
- Two weeks in October where I barely ran because of work (Beacon release)

What I learned:
- I need to eat more on long runs
- Easy days must be slow
- I run better when someone expects me, even if it is just a plan on the fridge

For 2025: a spring half, probably in Tartu with John, and somebody to run with. Also less phone on the evening run.
