# Iceland run 1: south coast

Woke up at 05:30 because the sun does not care about nights. Ran 7 km from the guesthouse in Vík toward the black sand beach and back, on a gravel road with sheep looking at me.

Wind from the sea was strong, about like running against a hair dryer. I took the first half into the wind and the second half was a gift. Pace was slow (6:30) but I did not care, I was looking at the cliffs.

Saw puffins from the path at one point, I had to stop. Maybe 20 seconds of looking is why the run took 46 minutes.

Back at the guesthouse (Triin still asleep) Elin's text: "ты жива?" I sent her a photo of the beach.
