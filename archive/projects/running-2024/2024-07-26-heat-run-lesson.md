# Heat lesson

Went out at 18:30 in 27 degrees for an easy 10 km and by km 6 I felt like a boiled potato. Heart rate 12 beats higher than normal for the same pace. Stopped at the water fountain by the playground, drank half a litre, poured the rest on my head, and walked for a minute.

Things to do differently:
- Go after 20:30 or before 07:00
- Take a bottle on every run longer than 8 km in summer
- Salty snack afterwards. I read that the headache is partly salt
- Slow down by 10-15 seconds per km without feeling bad about it

I finished at 6:25 pace, tired and a little ashamed. The kid at the playground said "run faster, auntie". I wish I had a good answer.
