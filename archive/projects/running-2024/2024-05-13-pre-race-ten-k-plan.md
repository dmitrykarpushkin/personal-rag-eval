# The 10k next Sunday

I signed up for a local 10k in Kadriorg as a practice race: flat, two laps around the park, nothing fancy. The point is to learn how a race morning works, not to win anything.

Plan:
- Breakfast two and a half hours before: oats, banana, coffee (small)
- Warm-up: 10 min easy plus 3 strides
- Start at 5:15/km for the first 3 km, then see
- Dream time: 52:xx, realistic: 53-54

My current 10k PB is from a trail race five years ago: 56 something. So any time under 55 counts as a win.

Reminder to self: do not start with the fast kids.
