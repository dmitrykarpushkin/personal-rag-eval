# Routes I actually use

**Seafront loop (8 km):** home -> Kalamaja promenade -> Stroomi beach -> back through Pelgulinn. Flat, windy, empty on weekdays. Good for easy runs.

**Kadriorg loop (10 km):** along the sea towards Kadriorg, the park (lap of the pond), then back via Pirita tee. Lit in the dark, has toilets in summer.

**Pirita out-and-back (14-19 km):** the long run option. The promenade ends in the beach, I turn there. Water at the kiosk by the marina, but only after 08:00.

**Paljassaare (12 km):** quiet, rough gravel and sandy bits. The wind is worse than anywhere. Birds in May.

Not worth it: Lasnamäe (hills and cars), the old town cobbles (ankle risk).
