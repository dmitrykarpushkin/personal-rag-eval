# Looking at the numbers a day later

Average pace 5:37/km, just under target. Second half only 14 seconds slower than the first, so I paced it reasonably. If I had held the first 5 km at 5:42 I might have done 1:57:30.

Heart rate: averaged 168, max 183 in the last 300 m. Comfortably hard, never red.

What I got right:
- Gels on time
- Not changing anything before the race
- Starting in a slower wave and passing people late

What to change:
- Warm-up: I did only 4 minutes. Take 10.
- Don't chase the first 3 km with the crowd
