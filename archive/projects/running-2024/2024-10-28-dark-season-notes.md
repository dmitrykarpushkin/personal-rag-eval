# Dark-season kit list

- Headlamp (the cheap one is fine, I lost the expensive one in Iceland)
- Reflective vest, because Estonian drivers do not expect runners at 18:30
- Thin gloves, thicker gloves for below zero, a buff
- Wool socks, two layers on top
- Yaktrax-style spikes for ice, one pair, kept in the hallway cupboard

The big thing is leaving the flat. I put the kit on the chair the night before and I do not decide in the morning. Decision fatigue is the enemy.

Today: 8 km in light rain, the first rain run that actually felt good.
