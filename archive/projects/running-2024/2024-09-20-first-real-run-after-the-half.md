First proper run since the half: 8 km at an easy 6:20 pace, legs light, no soreness. The air smelled of autumn: wet leaves and wood smoke from some garden in Pelgulinn.

No watch screen, only a lap button. I did not push once. Walked the last five minutes to cool off and ate an apple on the way home.

It is a different joy from the race: the one you do not tell anybody about.
