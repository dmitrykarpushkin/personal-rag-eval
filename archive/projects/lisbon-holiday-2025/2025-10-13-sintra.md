# Monday, Sintra

Early train, arrived before the buses. Pena palace is garish and fun, the gardens better than the palace. Walked down through the woods to the Moorish castle. Fog and then sun. Spent the afternoon at a quinta with a well and tunnels, exactly what I wanted.

Lunch was a cheese pastry on a wall. Home by six, fell asleep with my shoes on.
