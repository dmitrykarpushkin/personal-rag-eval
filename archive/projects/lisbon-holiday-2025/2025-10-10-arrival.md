# Friday, arrived

Landed in the evening, metro and then the hill to the flat. The stairs were as advertised. The host left a small jar of local jam, kind. Out for a walk at ten: warm, the streets full, music from three different bars. Ate grilled sardines at a counter, ridiculous good for €9.

First impression: the light even at night is soft. I felt like I had arrived somewhere I had been before.
