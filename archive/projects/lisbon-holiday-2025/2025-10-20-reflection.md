# After a week

I keep catching myself checking the Lisbon weather. Not good. Not terrible either.

What I liked: walking everywhere, the sea at the end of streets, running along the river, food that is simple, people who talk to strangers. What I did not like: the hills (my knees), the construction noise, the fact that I only saw it as a tourist, which is a pretty different thing.

Honest question: is this about Lisbon or about wanting to leave Lumen? Both, probably. Do not decide anything in October.
