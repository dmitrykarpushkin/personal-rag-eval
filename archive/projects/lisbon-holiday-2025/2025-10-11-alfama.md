# Saturday, Alfama and the old town

Up early, took tram 28 before the crowds, it was still crowded. Walked Alfama downhill from the castle for hours. Tiny courtyards, laundry, tiles. Ate a prego sandwich at a place the host recommended. Sunset at a miradouro with a guitar player and half the city.

Walked 24,000 steps according to the phone. My feet are done.
