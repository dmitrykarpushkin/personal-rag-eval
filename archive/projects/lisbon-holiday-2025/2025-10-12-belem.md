# Sunday, Belém

Went to Belém by tram, ate the famous pastéis de nata still warm, then another one, then regretted the second. The monastery was lovely, the tower I skipped. Long walk along the river, joggers everywhere. I noticed the running paths and made a mental note: could I run here? Yes, obviously.

Evening: fado in a tiny place with 20 seats, one singer, and a guitar. I did not understand a word and cried a bit anyway.
