# Why Lisbon

Reasons I wrote down on the balcony with a beer:
1. It is warm in October while Tallinn is 5 degrees and wet.
2. A colleague (Hanna) went and came back evangelical about the light and the food.
3. Flights are short enough for a long weekend plus.
4. I have never been to southern Europe by myself.

I do not want to turn this into a "life plan". It is five days. If I come home thinking about it more than I expect, I will let myself think about it. Not before.
