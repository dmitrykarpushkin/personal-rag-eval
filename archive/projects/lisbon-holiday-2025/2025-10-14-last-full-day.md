# Tuesday, last full day

A slow morning in the garden by the flat with the e-reader. In the afternoon an azulejo painting workshop in a small studio. Eight people, two hours, I painted a blue-and-white tile with a very wonky bird. The teacher said something about clay having "memory", and I thought about the mug in Iceland.

Dinner at a tasca with a handwritten menu, as planned. Octopus, a half bottle of white, and an old man at the next table who explained the menu to me in Portuguese, slowly. I understood about a third.
