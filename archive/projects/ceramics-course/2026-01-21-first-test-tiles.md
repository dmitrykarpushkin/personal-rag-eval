# First glaze test tiles

Anu keeps a tray of small flat tiles (about hand size) so we can test glazes before spending hours on a mug. I made twelve of them out of leftover clay, pressed with a texture on the left half and smooth on the right, and numbered them with the needle tool.

Plan:
- Tiles 1-3: celadon at one, two and three dips (thin, medium, thick)
- Tiles 4-6: tenmoku, same thing
- Tiles 7-9: satin white over a darker body (I used some dark clay for contrast)
- Tiles 10-12: layered, celadon over white, white over tenmoku, tenmoku over celadon

I wrote the plan in a notebook as well as on the shelf label, because I will otherwise forget which is which. Into the glaze kiln on Friday, ready the week after.

It is the first time since my school chemistry lab that I have kept a lab book.
