# Session 1: wedging and centering

Eight of us: me, Liina, Markus, Maarja, Rein (who has done it before and brags in a polite way), two women who came together, and a very quiet student in a cap.

Anu showed us how to wedge the clay: push, roll, push, roll, a "ram's head" shape, so that the air goes out. Then we were given 500 g balls and told to center them on the wheel. She said: "Elbows into your body. Be a rock."

I was not a rock. My first three balls ended up shaped like a mushroom, an egg, and nothing at all. The fourth stayed centred for four seconds and the room made a small sound.

By the end I could open the clay and make something that looked like a very short cup. Anu squashed it with a wire to show us the wall thickness ("too fat, but your hands are honest").

Hands trembling, clay in my hair. I want to go back already.
