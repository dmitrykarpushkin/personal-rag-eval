# Wheel rules to remember (from sessions 1-3)

1. Wedge properly. Air bubbles are surprises you do not want
2. Center fast, then slow down. Fast wheel to center, slower to pull
3. Keep your elbows tucked into your body or your thighs. Braced arms are quiet arms
4. Use less water. Sponge out the puddle in the bottom
5. Compress the base before pulling up the walls, otherwise it cracks in the kiln
6. Cut off the wheel with the wire, lift with wet hands
7. Mark the pot (initials) before it goes on the shelf, or you lose it among the others
8. Do not fall in love with your first pots

Anu also said: "Do not be afraid to destroy your pots. If you do not feel something is working, wedge it back up and start over. Clay is cheap, your attention is not."
