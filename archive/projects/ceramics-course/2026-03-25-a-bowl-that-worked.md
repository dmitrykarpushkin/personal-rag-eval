# The bowl that worked

A white satin bowl with a tenmoku rim. I thought the dark rim would be heavy, but it frames the white like an eyelid. The glaze fired clean, the foot is trimmed well, the walls are even, it sits flat.

It is the first thing I have made that I would be happy to show to a stranger. I put it on the windowsill in the Kalamaja flat and looked at it over breakfast for twenty minutes. Rebane (the landlord) came by to check the radiator and said "nice bowl". I said "I made it." He said "of course you did," in the voice of a man who cannot tell the difference between a bowl and a plate.

Anu came by with her tea, turned the bowl over and nodded. I am not sure which of us was more proud.
