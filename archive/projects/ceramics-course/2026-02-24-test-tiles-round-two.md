# Test tiles, round two

Wax-resist stripes under celadon. I painted vertical stripes of wax on twelve tiles (various widths) and dipped them in celadon at two and a half dips. The wax repels the glaze, leaving raw clay stripes that show the body colour.

Results after firing: the thin stripes are crisp and lovely; the wide ones look like a zebra crossing. Celadon pools slightly at the edges of the stripes, which makes them look drawn with ink. The white clay body looks clean and warm; the darker clay makes the contrast stronger.

Decision: thin stripes, two or three per mug, only on the lower third. I like it more than a plain glaze.

Photographed the tiles in a row on the windowsill. Anu: "this is a series now." Good news.
