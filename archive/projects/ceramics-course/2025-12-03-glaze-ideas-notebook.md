# Ideas notebook: glaze

I started a small notebook for glaze observations. Page one:

- Celadon: thin = pale and watery, thick = deep jade. Breaks to a rusty olive on edges. Looks best over a white body
- Tenmoku: dark, almost black, orange on rims. Looks serious
- Satin white: soft like a cloud; shows every finger mark
- Speckled (Anu's): freckles of iron, like toast

Question for next term: why is one celadon blue and another green? Anu says "the iron and the kiln atmosphere." I wrote it down as if I understood.

Each glaze gets a page, a tile, and a note on what it likes. Treat it like a friend: remember the birthdays.
