Anu looked at the seven mugs from last week, one by one, with her glasses on top of her head.

- "Handles: you are rushing the shape at the top. Let it breathe."
- "Rim: nice. You have the compression in your fingers now."
- "Weight: two are too heavy. A mug should feel like it wants to be lifted."
- "Glaze: the celadon is your colour. Keep it."
- "Bottom: the foot rings are the best I have seen from a first-year student."

I said "thank you" and she said "do not thank me, make forty more."

On the way home I realised that I have been smiling for twenty minutes, on the tram, in a queue and in the shop.
