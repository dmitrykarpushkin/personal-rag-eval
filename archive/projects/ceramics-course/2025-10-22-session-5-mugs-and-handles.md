# Session 5: mugs and handles

Back from Lisbon, brown and sleepy. Anu said I had missed nothing, then handed me the most stubborn cylinder in the class.

Today: mugs. Throw a cylinder with a slightly narrower top, leave the bottom a bit thicker, form a lip, cut a clean foot. The handle: pull a strip of clay from a lump (a bit like milking), shape it, attach it when leather hard, with a bit of slip.

Throwing: my first mug was too tall. The second was close to a nice mug. A third was a flowerpot. I kept two.

Handles are hard. Mine looked like drooping ears. Maarja's were elegant, like the ears of a fox. Anu said, "You pull, you pause, you let it hang, you do not pet it." I had to leave it for a week anyway because handles go on after the clay stiffens.

I want to learn how people make five identical ones in an hour.
