# Results: test tiles

Kiln opened on Thursday evening, I picked mine up on Friday.

- **Celadon:** one dip is almost invisible, two dips gives a delicate pale blue-green, three dips is lovely and glassy but runs on vertical edges. Two and a half is perfect
- **Tenmoku:** one dip is a thin brown, two is almost black with orange breaks at the edges, three is a puddle of black. Not for the lip of a mug, great for the inside of a bowl
- **Satin white:** soft, matte, buttery. On the dark clay it turns grey-ish and a bit oatmeal
- **Layered:** celadon over white is gorgeous. White over tenmoku looks like frost. Tenmoku over celadon: a mess

Wrote everything on the back of each tile with a marker. I keep the tiles pinned in a row on the wall above my desk, which looks like I am a scientist, or a kid.

Decision for the next batch: celadon, two and a half dips, for mugs and small bowls.
