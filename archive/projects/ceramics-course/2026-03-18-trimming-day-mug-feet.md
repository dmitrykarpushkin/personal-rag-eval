# Trimming ten mugs

Ten mugs at leather hard on the bench. Trimmed each foot on the wheel with a loop tool: remove a ring from the bottom, make a little step, compress the edge.

I did a few too thin (two of them will probably dry crack), but most are good. The sound the tool makes on leather-hard clay is the sound I like most in the world.

A visiting student asked how long it took me to learn. I said "six months, three hundred mugs and one teacher who never lies." True, except it was four months and about forty mugs.

Signed the bottoms ML 26 and put them on the shelf. Back on Friday to check dryness.
