Clay dries the skin on the hands badly. Things I learned from Liina and the internet:
- Cream before the class, not after (a thin layer protects)
- Use a nail brush and a lot of warm water at the studio sink, never in the flat sink, or the drain will turn into concrete
- Short nails, always
- A small piece of cotton to wrap around a cracked fingertip

My right thumb has a split. I am blaming the cold wheel water. Running has a similar rule: take care of the small things or the small things take over.
