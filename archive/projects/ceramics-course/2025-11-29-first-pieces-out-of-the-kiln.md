# The first batch is out

Picked up my pieces at Savi at noon. Anu had laid them out on a long table with name cards. Mine were at the end: five pieces, two of which look like something a person could use.

1. **Celadon mug.** Pale blue-green, slightly glossy, a faint runny drip on the side that turned out to be charming. Handle a bit crooked, I will pretend it is deliberate
2. **White-and-celadon bowl.** Lovely. The glaze pooled in the bottom, a deeper green
3. **Wide fruit bowl** with the hairline crack: it survived! The crack is a barely visible line in the foot. Anu: "your first scar"
4. **Two small bowls**: ugly, charming, will become jewellery dishes
5. **Lopsided mug**: leaks slightly; flowerpot

Photos taken on the table next to the window. I carried the box home in my lap on the tram like it was a cake.

Drinking coffee from the celadon mug. It is warm and the handle works. This is a new kind of happiness.
