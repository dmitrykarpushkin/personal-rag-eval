# Session 8: last class

We threw whatever we wanted for the final two hours. I made three small bowls and a mug that, for the first time, had walls that were even enough that Anu lifted it and said "good, this is a cup". I want it on a plaque.

Rein asked if we could continue next term. Anu mentioned that Savi has a studio membership with a shelf and open evenings and a kiln schedule, and that those of us who liked it could join. I said "yes" before she finished the sentence.

At the end we cleaned all the wheels and drank cheap sparkling wine out of mismatched cups, all made by students from older courses. Mine was a small thick one with a leaning handle. I will get my own pieces in December.

Eight Wednesdays gone. I do not know what to do with Wednesdays now.
