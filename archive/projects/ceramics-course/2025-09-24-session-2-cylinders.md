# Session 2: cylinders

The homework from last week was to watch videos; I did not. Anu knew.

Today's goal: a straight cylinder with an even wall. Opening, compressing the base, pulling the walls up in three pulls, then shaping the rim with a chamois.

My first cylinder collapsed on the second pull. The second one stood up and leaned like the tower of Pisa. The fifth was pretty good, or at least nobody said anything. I cut it in half with the wire to look at the profile: thick at the bottom, thin at the top, the classic.

Learned: water is the enemy. Use less than you think, and wipe the pooling out of the inside. Slow wheel speed when pulling up, fast when centring.

Markus has the talent: his cylinders look like machine-made. We all hate him fondly.
