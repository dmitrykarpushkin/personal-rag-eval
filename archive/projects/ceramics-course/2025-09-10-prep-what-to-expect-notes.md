# Before the first class

Things I looked up:
- A "wheel" is an electric turntable with a foot pedal; you control the speed with your foot
- Stoneware clay fires to about 1250 degrees in two stages: bisque first, then the glaze firing
- Clay shrinks about 12 percent from wet to fired, so pots end up smaller than they felt
- Hands need to be wet, but not drenched

Videos watched: five, one of which was 40 minutes of a man in Japan making the same cup in silence. Beautiful.
