# Session 4: trimming

Our bowls from last week were leather hard, which means firm enough to hold, soft enough to carve. We turned them upside down on the wheel, stuck them with little bits of clay and trimmed the foot with loop tools.

Trimming is calming. Shavings fly off like curly ribbons and you shave the extra weight away. I took too much from one bowl and went through the bottom with a pop. Rein said "everyone does it once". A second bowl survived and now has a neat little foot ring.

Anu showed how to compress the rim with a finger so it does not chip. She also showed how to sign the bottom. I wrote "ML 25".

The wide bowl is the first piece I like. I might keep it for fruit. Or for keys. Or I put nothing in it, and look at it.

Next Wednesday there is no class (Anu's autumn break), which suits me, because I am in Lisbon. Anu wrapped my pieces in plastic to stop them cracking while drying.
