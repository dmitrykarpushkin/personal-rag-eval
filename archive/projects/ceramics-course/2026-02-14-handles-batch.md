# Handles: batch of seven

Pulled seven handles on the bench, a long one with a roll, then curved around a bottle to dry a little while the mugs got ready. Attached them with slip: score the contact points, add slip, press, smooth the join with a wet finger.

Observation: the more calmly I pull, the nicer the shape. If I think about the shape the handle goes stiff. The two I did at the end, when I was hungry and not thinking, are the best. Same as running: the best kilometres are the ones you forget.

A small crack on one handle where it meets the mug. I patched it with a bit of slip. Anu says "pottery is long, patient maintenance."

All seven under a plastic bag for three days. If they dry slowly, they live.
