# Seven mugs out of the kiln

Six of them survived. One cracked at the handle (as feared).

The celadon ones, fired on Tuesday, are the best so far. The glaze is the right thickness, a milky, pale green with a darker line where it pools at the base. The handle shape is a bit different on each one, which I now call "hand-made character."

A cup test: pour boiling water, hold, check for leaks, check for heat on the handle. All fine. Drank a cup of tea from one. It felt like a quiet, small victory.

Noted: I will wait to decide what to do with them. I could give three to Elin and mum. Maybe one for John. I do not want to count them as products yet; I want to like them first.
