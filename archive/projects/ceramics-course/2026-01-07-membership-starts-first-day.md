# First day as a Savi member

Joined the studio today. A member gets: a shelf for work in progress (a labelled spot on the rack at the back, mine is "M. Lindqvist, shelf 4"), a locker for tools, access to the open evenings and also weekday mornings, and a firing schedule: Anu loads the kiln on Fridays and the member pieces are fired in turn.

Rules on the whiteboard:
- Wipe the wheel after use. Wipe the floor under the wheel as well
- Reclaim your scraps: there is a bucket for clay slip and a drying board
- Sign up for firings on the board. A piece without a name is a piece that gets thrown away
- Tea and coffee free, biscuits donated

I bought my own set of tools: a wooden rib, a metal rib, a loop tool, a pin tool, a sponge on a stick. The cashier looked at me like a rookie. That is correct.

Threw four cylinders and six small bowls. It is good to have a place.
