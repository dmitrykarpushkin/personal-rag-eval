Settling into a routine at Savi on the weekday mornings:

- 10:30 arrive, tea, check the board for firings
- Wedge 6-8 balls of clay, 350-500 g each (about twenty minutes, and the best warm-up for my shoulders)
- Throw until about 13:00
- Clean up: sponge the wheel, rinse the splash pan, put the leftover clay in the reclaim bucket
- Trim yesterday's pieces if they are leather hard

Anu said that reclaim is "the quiet hero of every studio". It is the grey bucket where everyone's failed pots and dry trimmings go to become clay again. A bit like a good workout log: nothing is wasted.

First week of January I made 31 pieces. Seven survived the first inspection.
