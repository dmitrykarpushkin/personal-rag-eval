# Mug sketches

Drew twelve mug shapes in a notebook on the sofa, mostly in ballpoint pen, mostly wrong:

1. Straight, classic, thick walls
2. Slightly flared at the lip, narrow at the base
3. Wide and low, a "tea cup" with no handle
4. Tall, with a thumb rest on the handle
5. Cup with a pinched spout (probably a bad idea)
6. A rounded belly with a short neck, like a bean
...

I like number 2 and number 4. I am not sure what makes a good mug yet: the weight, the way the lip feels on the mouth, how the handle sits against the fingers. The best ones I own are not the prettiest. I want to know why.

Anu says "make a hundred and then you will know." Okay.
