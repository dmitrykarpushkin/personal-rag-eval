# Open evening: throwing sets

Goal for the night: ten mugs of the same size, from 350 g of clay each. I weighed the clay with a small kitchen scale. Anu looked at it and said "you have become a person who weighs clay." I could not argue.

I managed seven. The first three were tall, the next ones got shorter, the last two were exactly right. Everyone says the muscle memory comes with repetition and I felt it start to come somewhere around mug number five: the hand knew where to stop.

Liina threw twelve bowls in the same time. She is a machine, but she still talks to every bowl.

Wrapped the mugs in plastic and went home at ten. Handles tomorrow, when they are leather hard.
