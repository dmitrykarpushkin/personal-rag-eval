# Glaze thickness: my cheat sheet

From Anu, from the test tiles and from ruining a mug:

- Count seconds, but also count the weight: a pot that is dry and bisqued is thirsty, so it takes the glaze fast. A damp or warm one, slower
- Stir the bucket before each dip. The heavy stuff sinks to the bottom
- Two dips of three seconds each, not one dip of six
- Check the glaze thickness with a fingernail: a slight scratch should show a coat as thick as a business card
- If it looks too thin, do not try to fix it with a brush afterwards. Re-dip in a day or so
- A glaze that is too thick pools at the bottom and runs onto the shelf. That is how you lose a shelf and a friendship

Ruined mug: three dips of celadon, a big smudge near the handle where my fingers touched. Sanded the smudge, re-glazed the spot and hoped. We will see in a week.
