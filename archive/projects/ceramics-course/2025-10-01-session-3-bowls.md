# Session 3: bowls

We moved on to bowls. Anu said bowls are cylinders that have learned to relax. She did a demonstration in about forty seconds and said "like that" and everyone laughed.

My first bowl: too thick at the rim, too thin at the foot ring area, and a wobble I cannot explain. The second one: it stayed centred, the curve was nearly even, and I felt something I have only felt before at the end of a good run.

I kept two for trimming next week: a wide shallow one and a smaller deeper one. Marked them with my initials on the bottom with a needle tool, ML in tiny crooked letters.

My forearms ache. This is a sport.
