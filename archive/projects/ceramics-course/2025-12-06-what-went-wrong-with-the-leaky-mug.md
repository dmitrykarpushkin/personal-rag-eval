# Why does the lopsided mug leak?

Asked Anu on Thursday. She turned it over, held it to the light and said: "Thin spot near the foot, probably a pinhole in the glaze, and a tiny crack that opened when it dried too fast."

Causes (in order of likelihood):
1. I compressed the base too little, so it cracked while drying
2. I left it near the radiator (my fault)
3. The glaze was too thin in that spot

What to do next time: compress the bottom with a rib, dry slowly under plastic, and check the glaze on the inside of the cup before it goes in the kiln.

It is a flowerpot now. A succulent lives there. It is happy.
