# Why I signed up for a pottery course

For about a year I have been picking up mugs at flea markets and turning them over to look at the bottoms. At some point you have to try making one.

Savi is a shared ceramics studio in Telliskivi, run by Anu Põld. They have a beginner wheel course in autumn: eight Wednesday evenings, 18:30 to 21:00, small group (eight people), clay and firing included. I paid and I am on the list.

Honest motives:
- I sit all day at a laptop and want something where the result is a physical object
- Running is my body hobby, I want one for my hands
- Anu's studio looked clean and calm in the photos, and the Instagram has no "inspirational" quotes

Expectation: the first three weeks will produce lopsided lumps. I am prepared.
