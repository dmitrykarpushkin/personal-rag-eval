Morning after the first class: forearms sore, a faint grey line of clay under every fingernail, and a patch of dry skin on my right palm that I will need to treat like a runner's blister. Bought hand cream on the way to the tram.

I kept thinking about the centred ball of clay at the end of the night, the moment when it stopped wobbling under my hands. A ten-second feeling that I would like to have more often, in other areas of life too.

Oskar once told me that you learn a craft by doing it badly for a very long time. That is going to be my motto for the next few months.
