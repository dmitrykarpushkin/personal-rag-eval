# Notes on waxing feet

Every pot gets a clean, unglazed foot or it welds itself to the kiln shelf. Wax resist on the bottom and about 5 mm up the side. A foam brush works. Sponge the bottom after dipping in glaze anyway, because the wax never covers everything.

Today Anu caught me waxing a foot a little high, so the glaze stopped just short of the line and left a bare ring on the mug like a collar. It looks funny and a bit wrong. "A collar is not a mistake," she said, "it is an unplanned decoration."

Ideas for later: waxing patterns on the outside, stripes or dots, so the raw clay shows through.

I will test it next week on a tile.
