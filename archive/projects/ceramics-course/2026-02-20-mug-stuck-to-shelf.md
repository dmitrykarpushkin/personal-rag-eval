# The mug that fused to the shelf

Opened the glaze kiln on Thursday and one of my celadon mugs was welded to the shelf by a puddle of glass. I had waxed the foot, but not high enough, and I had dipped it too thick at the bottom, so the glaze ran down and glued the mug to the kiln furniture.

Anu spent twenty minutes with a small chisel and a face mask removing it, and she was not angry, only slightly disappointed in the way a teacher can be. The mug came out with a rough chunk missing from its foot. The shelf was ground smooth.

"Everybody does it once," she said. "Now you have done yours."

Rule from now on: leave 4-5 mm of bare clay above the foot, sponge the bottom edge, never dip thick near the base. Wrote it on the shelf label in marker: "WAX THE FOOT HIGHER, MARA".
