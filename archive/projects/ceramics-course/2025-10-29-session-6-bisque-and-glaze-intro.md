# Session 6: bisque and an introduction to glazes

Our first bowls came back from the bisque firing: pale, chalky, a little lighter than I expected, absolutely identical in colour so you cannot judge anything. Mine had one hairline crack in the wide bowl's foot. Anu said "it will probably be fine." I can hear "probably".

Then glazes. Anu has a shelf of buckets: a clear, a satin white, a celadon (the pale blue-green), a rich brown called tenmoku, and a speckled one she makes herself. The colours in the bucket do not look like the fired colours, which is the first great deception of ceramics.

She showed us dipping and brushing. Dip for 3 seconds, no more. Wax resist on the foot so the pot does not weld itself to the shelf. Wipe the bottom with a sponge.

Homework: choose two glazes for next week and write down why. I chose celadon for the mug (a cup should look cold and calm) and the white for the bowl.
