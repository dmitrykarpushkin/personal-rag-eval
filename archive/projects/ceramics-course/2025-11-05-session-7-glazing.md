# Session 7: glazing day

I glazed my first two pieces, a mug and a small bowl.

- Mug: celadon, dipped for 3 seconds, one drip on the side. I wiped it and I made it worse. Anu said "leave it, the glaze will flow in the kiln and may hide it"
- Bowl: white on the outside, celadon inside, a bit of wax on the rim so it stays raw there

Rein glazed in a bold speckled brown and I was jealous. Maarja used the wax to draw tiny dots. Liina made a bowl that looked like a moon.

Everything goes into the kiln next week. It will come out in two weeks. Anu says it is like Christmas for adults.

I took a photo of my mug before it went into the kiln. It is a pale grey-green dust now. I will compare it with the result.
