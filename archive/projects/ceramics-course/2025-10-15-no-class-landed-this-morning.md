No class tonight (Anu's autumn break), which is lucky, because I landed at breakfast time in grey Tallinn rain and have been horizontal on the sofa ever since.

Yesterday in Lisbon I painted a blue-and-white tile at a small azulejo workshop and a man there told me clay has a memory. I keep thinking about it. A tile in my bag, wrapped in a jumper, and a bowl waiting under plastic at Savi. The two of them probably do not know each other.

Anu said before I left: "Go look at the tiles. Your bowls will dry slowly under the plastic, don't worry." I did look. I will tell her next week.
