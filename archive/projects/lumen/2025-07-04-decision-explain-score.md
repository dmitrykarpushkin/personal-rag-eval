# Decision: Every score must come with a reason

Principle for Beacon v2: no score without a visible explanation in plain words. Applies to the risk list and the partner view. Cost: about two sprints. Benefit: CS managers can use the score in customer calls. Kertu liked it enough to put it in the roadmap deck.
