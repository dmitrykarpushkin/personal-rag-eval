# Decision: Dropping the churn probability number

Pilot users do not trust the probability number and two CS leads said they ignore it. Decision: remove it from the UI before the beta, keep the underlying model out of production until we can explain it. Oskar signed off. Reason in one line: a number nobody trusts teaches people to ignore the whole page.
