# Telling the landlord I am going freelance

I wrote to Mart so he does not hear it from someone else: from January I will be working for myself, I will be at home more, rent will be paid as before, and I can share a bank statement or other proof of income if he needs it. I wanted to be honest.

He answered kindly: he does not need any papers, he has had no problem with me in two years, and he wished me luck. He also suggested the radiator in the room I plan to use as an office be checked before winter gets serious.

Moved the desk to the window.
