# Email: Tallinn half marathon, race information

My start wave: **B**, 09:00 sharp, behind the elite and sub-1:45 runners. Number to be collected at the expo at the Song Festival Grounds on Friday (12:00-19:00) or Saturday (10:00-17:00).

Weather warning: they recommend checking the forecast, mention that the finish area will have a hot soup tent and showers in the nearby sports hall.

Course notes in the PDF: start on the street by the port, a loop through the city and along the promenade, finishing at the Song Festival Grounds. The only hill is the bridge at around km 16.

One thing: the organiser reminds people to bring their own cup. Plastic cups at the water stations are out of fashion.
