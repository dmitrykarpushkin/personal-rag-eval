# Hanna's farewell and what I learned

Hanna's last Thursday lunch, with half the floor.

She gave me two things: a list of Berlin contacts 'in case', and a sentence I am keeping: 'You are the person who notices.' I asked her what I notice. She said everything, and then you hold it until it hurts.

Kertu asked me later whether I am okay. I said yes. I partly meant it.
