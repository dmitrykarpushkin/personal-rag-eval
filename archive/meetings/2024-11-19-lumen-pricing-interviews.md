# Sales interviews for the pricing page

Two interviews, 30 minutes each, with sales reps Rain and Kaisa.

Common complaints: people cannot tell which plan they need; the table breaks on mobile; the annual discount is invisible.
Kaisa wants a calculator. Rain says calculators are a trap, customers never trust them.
They agree on this: the 'contact sales' button should not be the only way to get an answer.

I wrote down their exact words for the recommendation.
