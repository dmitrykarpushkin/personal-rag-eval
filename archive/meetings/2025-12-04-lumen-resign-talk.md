# Conversation with Kertu about leaving

Kertu and me, closed room, 45 minutes, the day after I told her.

She asked what would have to be different. I said it is not about Lumen, it is about wanting to build my own thing and be paid by more than one company.
She asked if I would consider part-time or a contract. I said I would consider contracting later but not as an exit hatch.
We agreed a clean handover and a goodbye lunch for the team, no big announcement until Monday.

Honest note: it was kinder than I deserved and also easier than I feared.
