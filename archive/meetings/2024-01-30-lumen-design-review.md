# Design review, account list

Short call with the design lead and Karl. We looked at three mockups of the account list.

Chosen: the dense one with a coloured score chip and a small trend line. The airy one looked nicer in the file and worse with 400 rows.

Design lead wants the chip colours to pass contrast checks, so the red gets darker. Fine by me.

Open: what do we show for accounts with fewer than 14 days of data? Proposal: grey chip, 'not enough data'.
