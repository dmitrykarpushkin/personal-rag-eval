# Call with Nordvik Retail about Atlas beta

Customer call, 30 minutes, with Kertu and Nordvik's data lead.

They export from an old ERP: semicolons, comma decimals, dates like 3.12.24. Showed how mapping handles it. They liked the undo.

Their wish list: scheduled imports from SFTP, an API, and a template so their team stops remapping columns every Monday. All three are on our list, in that order.

They are willing to be a reference. Kertu will ask them again after GA.
